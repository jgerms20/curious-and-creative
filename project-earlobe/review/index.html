The part to plan around

A verified 7-minute local brief costs about $3.91 in AI and voice. On ads alone it breaks even near 455 downloads an episode, and research puts a new local show nearer 120. Local sponsors, Studio Services and B2B carry the early months. Keep about $25k of runway for the conservative case.

From a press release to your feed

  G -- "PUBLISH" --> V["Studio

AI disclosure"]
  G -- "REWRITE" --> P
  G -- "HOLD (breaking)" --> T
  G -- "KILL" --> X["Logged, not aired"]
  V --> F["Publisher
feed, notes, trust log"]

The gate is plain code, not a model. The same reports always produce the same decision. Breaking news waits until three independent outlets or an official source confirm it.
