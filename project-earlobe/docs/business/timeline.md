## Startup-time estimate

- **To first public episode:** about **3–4 weeks**. That's one week of setup and 2–3 weeks of dark run.
- **To the full test lineup (7 shows):** about **10–12 weeks**. The pace caps are deliberate, to stay out of AI-spam filters.
- **To Wave 1:** about **3–4 months**, depending on Gate A evidence.
