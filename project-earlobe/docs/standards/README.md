# Standards: the rules the network runs on

**The Curious & Creative Network publishes with no person reading each episode first, so the rules are written down, strict and checked by code. Start here.**

## The documents

| Doc | One line |
|---|---|
| [`editorial-standards.md`](editorial-standards.md) | The master rulebook: sourcing, accuracy, fairness, what we never do, lanes, corrections, and the gate rule map. Wins any conflict. |
| [`verification-protocol.md`](verification-protocol.md) | How the blind Source Verifier finds its own sources, counts independent owners and decides how much proof a claim needs. |
| [`fact-check-protocol.md`](fact-check-protocol.md) | How the Fact Checker rates every claim's exact wording against the evidence, claim type by claim type. |
| [`ai-disclosure.md`](ai-disclosure.md) | How we say we are AI: the end-of-episode scripts for each kind of show, the metadata text for each platform, and what to re-check as platform rules change. |

**Order for a newcomer:** Editorial Standards §1 to §2, then AI Disclosure, then the Voice & Persona Guide. The two protocols are for people changing the Trust Stack.

**If two pages disagree,** Editorial Standards win and the other page is fixed the same day. The code and these pages must say the same thing (`AUTO-9`). House rules for every doc are in [`../CONVENTIONS.md`](../CONVENTIONS.md). Numbers come from [`../research/facts-and-figures.md`](../research/facts-and-figures.md).

## Our 10 promises to listeners

| # | We promise | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **We'll tell you how we know.** Every episode's show notes list and link its sources. | `SRC-13`, `COPY-4` |
| **2** | **Two places, minimum. Three for breaking news.** A fact that cannot clear the bar is left out. | `SRC-1`, `BRK-2` |
| **3** | **We'll tell you we're AI, every time.** A spoken disclosure ends every episode, and the same words are in its details. | `VOICE-3`, `VOICE-5` |
| **4** | **We'll never fake a voice, a quote or a source.** Our hosts are original synthetic voices. Every quote is real, word for word, and credited. | `NEVER-4`, `QUO-1` |
| **5** | **We'll be fair, most of all to people who can't answer back:** people accused of crimes, victims and their families. | `FAIR-4`, `FAIR-6`, `FAIR-10` |
| **6** | **We'll say when we're wrong, publicly.** Corrections are dated, visible and never silent. | `AUTO-6`, `AUTO-7`, `NEVER-11` |
| **7** | **We'd rather be late, or skip a day, than be wrong.** A missing episode is acceptable. A wrong one is not. | `ACC-12`, `BRK-5` |
| **8** | **We won't name a child, or anyone who says they were sexually assaulted.** A hard stop in every show, with no exceptions today. | `NEVER-2`, `NEVER-3`, gate `L2` |
| **9** | **Advertisers pay for ad time, never for coverage.** Ads are marked, kept apart from the news, and never see a script. | `COI-1`, `COI-2`, `NEVER-9` |
| **10** | **A company answers for every episode, not "the AI."** Curious & Creative LLC is accountable, and no one can push an episode past the gate. | `AUTO-1`, `AUTO-2`, `NEVER-10` |

*Promises 1 to 7 are the list in Editorial Standards §1. Promises 8 to 10 come from §7, §12 and §15. Editorial Standards §1 still lists seven and needs the same three added (`AUTO-9`).*
