## The short version

1. **Startup cash.**
   - About **$7,300 one-time**: legal review, trademark, DBA, brand art and domain.
   - About **$1,400 a month** to run the test markets: 7 shows, about 125 episodes a month.
2. **Cost per episode falls fast with scale:**

   | Stage | All-in cost per episode |
   |---|---|
   | Test | $11.44 |
   | Wave 1 | $6.36 |
   | Scale | $3.78 |
   | Dominate | $2.86 |

   Fixed costs (insurance, data, hosting) dominate early. Claude work plus voice runs about $2–$9 an episode depending on the format.
3. **Ads alone don't pay for a local daily brief yet.**
   - At the researched base case (120 downloads per episode, $13 CPM, 55% fill), a 7-minute verified brief costs about $3.91 in Claude plus voice.
   - It only breaks even at about **455 downloads per episode** from ads.
   - Local sponsors, community packages and the B2B and services lines carry the early months. This matches what the Inception Point teardown shows: volume plus programmatic ads alone is thin.
4. **Base case:**
   - Payback in **month 7**.
   - Peak cash needed about **$9,300**.
   - Year-1 revenue about **$237k** against about **$72k** total cost.
   - **Network-only** (ads, sponsors and premium, with no B2B, API or services) is still positive for year 1 at about +$47k. It needs about $13.9k of cash before it turns.
5. **Conservative case:**
   - Payback in month 9, with about $11.7k of cash needed.
   - The **network alone loses about $25k in year 1**. Services and B2B are what keep it alive.
   - Plan cash for this case, and treat everything above it as upside.

How to read the table:

  - Flash-class models: about half the credits (F-TTS-09).

## 5. Twelve-month ROI

The default stage path is months 1–3 Test, 4–6 Wave 1, and 7–12 Scale. Every show is charged its full cost from launch, and only revenue follows the audience ramp. Both the path and the ramp are editable on Assumptions.

| | Conservative | Base | Optimistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Payback month (cumulative cash turns positive) | 9 | **7** | 5 |
| Peak cash needed before payback | −$11,683 | **−$9,326** | −$8,156 |
| Year-1 revenue | $102,706 | **$236,757** | $446,267 |
| Year-1 total cost (incl. one-time) | $71,763 | $71,763 | $71,763 |
| Year-1 ROI (net ÷ cost) | 0.43× | **2.3×** | 5.0× |
| Network-only: peak cash needed | −$26,426 | −$13,850 | −$10,539 |
| Network-only: year-1 cumulative net | −$24,856 | **+$46,644** | +$166,529 |

**Honest read:**

- The network makes money in the base case.
- The conservative case shows how fragile ad-only economics are at small audiences. The same thing pushed Inception Point toward selling infrastructure.
- The Studio Services and B2B lines are real hedges, not decoration. Budget about **$25k of runway** to cover the conservative case.

## 7. The levers that matter most

Ranked by impact on network-only net:

1. **Downloads per episode.** Every +100 downloads per local brief adds about $0.86 of ad revenue per episode (base). That's why distribution and growth agents exist.
2. **Local sponsor sell-through.** Going from 25% to 50% of local slots sold roughly doubles sponsor revenue.
3. **Voice cost.** Flash-class models, the API or the grant could cut voice 40–60%.
4. **Verifier efficiency.** Caching and smarter search budgets on the blind verifier. Measure real tokens per gate after launch.
5. **Show mix.** Breaking and archive episodes break even at the lowest download counts per dollar. Local briefs and courts need sponsors.
