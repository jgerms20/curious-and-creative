# Multi-Agent Workflow Examples

Real-world examples of how to orchestrate multiple agents for common marketing scenarios.

---

## Example 1: Blog Post with Research

### Request
"Research content marketing trends for 2026 and create a comprehensive blog post with supporting graphics"

### Multi-Agent Workflow

#### Phase 1: Research & Insights (Data Strategist)
**Task:** Analyze content marketing trends, search data, and audience interests
**Deliverables:**
- Trend analysis report
- Search volume data for key topics
- Audience insights and pain points
**Duration:** Day 1

#### Phase 2: Brand & Messaging (Brand Strategist)
**Task:** Define the brand's POV and angle on these trends
**Deliverables:**
- Brand perspective on trends
- Key messages to emphasize
- Tone and voice guidelines
**Dependencies:** Data Strategist findings
**Duration:** Day 1-2

#### Phase 3: Content Creation (Copywriter)
**Task:** Write the blog post incorporating research and brand messaging
**Deliverables:**
- 1,500-word blog post
- SEO-optimized headlines
- Meta description
**Dependencies:** Brand Strategist messaging
**Duration:** Day 2-3

#### Phase 4: Visual Design (Art Director)
**Task:** Create supporting visuals and graphics
**Deliverables:**
- Featured image
- 3-4 infographics for key data points
- Social sharing graphics
**Dependencies:** Copywriter draft
**Duration:** Day 3-4

#### Phase 5: Distribution Optimization (Communication Strategist)
**Task:** Optimize for SEO and plan distribution
**Deliverables:**
- SEO recommendations
- Social media distribution plan
- Email promotion strategy
**Dependencies:** Completed content
**Duration:** Day 4

### Timeline: 4-5 days
### Agents Used: 5

## Example 2: Product Launch Campaign

### Request
"Launch our new eco-friendly water bottle line targeting Gen Z with a $250K budget"

### Multi-Agent Workflow

#### Phase 1: Brand Foundation (Brand Strategist)
**Task:** Establish brand positioning for the product line
**Deliverables:**
- Product positioning statement
- Key differentiators vs. competitors
- Messaging framework
- Brand personality for this line
**Duration:** Week 1

#### Phase 2: Channel Strategy (Communication Strategist)
**Task:** Design media strategy and channel mix
**Deliverables:**
- Recommended channel mix (TikTok, Instagram, influencer, experiential)
- Budget allocation by channel
- Timeline and flight dates
- Platform-specific recommendations
**Dependencies:** Brand positioning
**Duration:** Week 1

#### Phase 3: Measurement Planning (Data Strategist)
**Task:** Define success metrics and tracking
**Deliverables:**
- KPI framework
- Measurement plan
- Dashboard requirements
- Testing roadmap
**Duration:** Week 1

#### Phase 4: Creative Concepts (Copywriter + Art Director)
**Task:** Develop integrated creative campaign
**Deliverables:**
- 3 campaign concept directions
- Headlines and taglines
- Visual mood boards
- Sample executions (social, display, OOH)
**Dependencies:** Brand and channel strategy
**Duration:** Week 2

#### Phase 5: Production Planning (Production Manager)
**Task:** Plan production for approved creative
**Deliverables:**
- Production timeline
- Vendor recommendations
- Cost estimates
- Asset specifications
**Dependencies:** Approved creative
**Duration:** Week 3

#### Phase 6: Campaign Coordination (Account Manager)
**Task:** Coordinate execution across all workstreams
**Deliverables:**
- Master project timeline
- Stakeholder communication plan
- Risk management
- Budget tracking
**Duration:** Week 2-8 (ongoing)

#### Phase 7: Launch Preparation (All Agents)
**Task:** Final review and launch preparation
**Deliverables:**
- All assets finalized and delivered
- Tracking implemented
- Team briefings completed
- Launch runbook
**Duration:** Week 7-8

### Timeline: 8 weeks
### Budget: $250K
### Agents Used: 7 (all agents)

## Key Patterns Across Workflows

### Pattern 1: Strategy Before Execution
- Always start with Brand Strategist and/or Communication Strategist
- Define "what to say" and "where to say it" first
- Then move to creative execution

### Pattern 2: Parallel Work Opportunities
- Data analysis can happen alongside brand strategy
- Copy and art direction can work in parallel (after strategy)
- Multiple agents can work simultaneously when dependencies allow

### Pattern 3: Account Manager as Coordinator
- Bring in Account Manager for complex, multi-phase projects
- They coordinate timelines, approvals, and communication
- Essential for client-facing work

### Pattern 4: Measurement from the Start
- Engage Data Strategist early in campaigns
- Define KPIs before launching
- Plan measurement framework upfront

### Pattern 5: Documentation Mindset
- Awards Agent captures work in real-time
- Don't wait until awards season
- Case studies become much easier
