About us

Two curious minds, one creative hub.

Curious & Creative is the home Joshua German and Janel Moore built for everything they keep making — the podcasts, the videos, the photography, the inventions, and the work that doesn't fit one job description.

Why we exist

We chase the stories and ideas that make culture more curious.

Curious & Creative is the umbrella where we launch podcasts, build ventures, and take on creative work without forcing any of it into the wrong container. The hub exists so the work has room to breathe — and to grow into whatever shape it actually wants to take.

That flexibility matters when you pair a strategist with a magpie brain (Joshua) with a policy storyteller with a microphone (Janel). Different lenses, shared instinct: follow the interesting thing until it becomes something real.

Both of us are University of South Carolina grads — Joshua in Mass Communications, Janel in Global Studies. Shared roots turned into shared ambition.
