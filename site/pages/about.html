Our story

Same curiosity. Different directions.

We met at the University of South Carolina and have been swapping ideas ever since. Underneath everything we make is the same instinct: notice what's interesting, dig until we actually understand it, then make something other people can feel.

That instinct takes each of us down different roads. It runs through brand strategy, photography, and AI builds; through policy, politics, finance, and technology; through long-form research, late-night debates, and art. Different expressions, same source.

Curious & Creative is where those roads meet: our flagship podcast and the shows we each host, the pictures and art we make, the games we invent, and a studio that brings the same curiosity to brands and founders.
