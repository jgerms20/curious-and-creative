Our story

Two curious minds. One canvas.

Curious & Creative is the studio Joshua German and Janel Moore built to hold everything they keep dreaming up — the podcasts, the ventures, the side quests, the work that doesn't fit any one job description.

Mission

We champion bold new voices and forge the stories that shift culture.

Curious & Creative exists to be the umbrella where Joshua and Janel can launch podcasts, ventures, and creative work without forcing them into the wrong container. A show doesn't have to pretend it's an agency. A venture doesn't have to pretend it's a side project. The studio holds all of it.

That flexibility matters when you have a strategist and a policy storyteller at the table. Joshua brings pattern recognition across brand, culture, and technology. Janel brings the political and social literacy to make a story land where it counts. The umbrella is what lets both ways of working show up on the same page.

Where it started

Two Gamecocks who couldn't stop building.

Both founders are University of South Carolina graduates. Joshua finished Mass Communications in 2020 with an Information Science minor, after coming up through the Gamecock Gateway program. Janel earned her BA in Global Studies from the same university.

South Carolina is where the habit started — the habit of taking on more than the job description, of building the next thing before anyone asked. Shared roots turned into shared ambition, and the studio is what that ambition looks like once it stops being a side conversation.
