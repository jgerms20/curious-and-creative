About us

Two curious minds, one little studio.

Curious & Creative is the studio Joshua German and Janel Moore built to hold everything they keep dreaming up — the podcasts, the ventures, the side quests, the work that doesn't fit one job description.

Why we exist

We chase the stories and ideas that make culture more curious.

Curious & Creative is the umbrella where we launch podcasts, build ventures, and take on creative work without forcing any of it into the wrong container. The studio exists so the work has room to breathe — and grow into whatever shape it actually wants to take.

That flexibility matters when you pair a strategist with a magpie brain (Joshua) and a policy storyteller with a microphone (Janel). Different lenses, shared instinct: follow the interesting thing until it becomes something real.
