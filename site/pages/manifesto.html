Our Standards

Start with a question.

Not the easy one. The one that makes the room go quiet.

Curiosity is where the work begins. Creativity is what we do with what we find.

Then back it up.

We check before we speak. We show our sources. We say what we know, and we say what we don't. Journalistic integrity is the floor, not the ceiling.

Taste is the job.

Anyone can make more. The work is knowing what deserves attention, which questions are worth chasing, and how an idea wants to be made.

AI amplifies our judgment.

We are AI-native by design. It widens the search and speeds up the draft. It never makes the call. A person does, and that person answers for it.

Attention is borrowed.

So we give something back: a fact, a feeling, a laugh, a reason to look again.

Shows. Photographs. Strategies. Things that didn't exist yesterday.

Curious first. Then creative.

On the Network

How the Network holds to it.

Every show on the Curious & Creative Network is held to these.

  1. Accuracy is the product.Every fact is checked against a source before it airs. If we cannot verify it, we say so, or we do not say it.
  2. We correct ourselves in public.When we get something wrong, the correction runs where the error ran.
  3. We report where reporting is missing.Niches, towns, and beats nobody else covers. We are not taking reporters' jobs; we are covering ground that has been left uncovered.
  4. We say what we know and what we do not.Alleged means alleged. Charged does not mean convicted.
  5. Entertainment is a principle, not a compromise.A show nobody enjoys informs nobody.
  6. Humans set the standard.Our shows are made with modern tools, including AI, and every one is directed by and accountable to a person.
  7. Curiosity is the method.Range across subjects is the focus, not a lack of it.
  8. Anyone can ask for a show.If a community needs coverage and nobody is making it, ask us.
Every regular episode ends the same way
