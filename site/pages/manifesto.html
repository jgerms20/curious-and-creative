Manifesto

Accurate. Curious. Worth your time.

The Curious + Creative Network exists to bring people accurate information, steeped in journalistic principles, with entertainment as a real principle too. We report in the niches, towns, and beats nobody else covers. It is a respectable, needed service, and these are the standards we hold it to.

  1. Accuracy is the product.Every fact is checked against a source before it airs. If we cannot verify it, we say so, or we do not say it.
  2. We correct ourselves in public.When we get something wrong, the correction runs where the error ran.
  3. We report where reporting is missing.Niches, towns, and beats nobody else covers. We are not taking reporters' jobs; we are covering ground that has been left uncovered.
  4. We say what we know and what we do not.Alleged means alleged. Charged does not mean convicted.
  5. Entertainment is a principle, not a compromise.A show nobody enjoys informs nobody.
  6. Humans set the standard.Our shows are made with modern tools, including AI, and every one is directed by and accountable to a person.
  7. Curiosity is the method.Range across subjects is the focus, not a lack of it.
  8. Anyone can ask for a show.If a community needs coverage and nobody is making it, ask us.
Every regular episode ends the same way
