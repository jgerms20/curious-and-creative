About this episode

Jack Dorsey has spent his career trying to make systems move faster, with fewer people standing in the way.

This episode traces that idea from his early fascination with emergency dispatch and city maps to the creation of Twitter and Square, where information and money could move with unprecedented speed. It looks at how that same instinct eventually pushed him away from centralized platforms and toward Bitcoin, Nostr, and open-source infrastructure.

We get into asceticism, systems design, decentralization, individual sovereignty, and Dorsey’s growing belief that the best technology is often the kind that disappears into the background. This is a story about flow, control, and one entrepreneur’s long attempt to build tools that institutions can’t easily shut down.
