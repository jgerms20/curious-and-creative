About this episode

He started as a kid from Chicago with dyslexia, ADHD, and a phone he wouldn't put down. Then Ari Emanuel got fired from his own job — and built Endeavor out of a walkout, turned a talent agency into a private-equity empire, and bought the UFC and the WWE along the way. This is the full story: the ICM walkout, the Silver Lake deal, the $2.4 billion IMG bet, the $4 billion UFC gamble, the $21.4 billion TKO combination, and the negotiation playbook — information, persistence, leverage — that made him the most feared man in Hollywood. We trace the three-act history of power from Wasserman to Ovitz to Emanuel, dissect the WGA war over packaging, separate the verified stories from the Hollywood lore, and ask the real question: what happens when the agent stops working for talent and starts owning the talent's world?
