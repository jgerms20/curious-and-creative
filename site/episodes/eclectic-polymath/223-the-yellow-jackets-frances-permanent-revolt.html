About this episode

Every car in France carried the uniform. The law put it there. In November 2018 the drivers of the periphery put it on, seized the roundabouts, and cancelled a fuel tax in eighteen days.

No leaders. No party. Nobody who could sign a peace. So how did a movement that could not negotiate win some €10 billion, freeze France's carbon tax for eight years and counting, and still elect nobody? The dress rehearsals in red caps. The parliament of traffic circles. The eye injuries and the €135 vest. And why the vests came back out of the trunk in October 2026, this time marching behind students.
