About this episode

Professional sports can look like pure competition on the field, but behind every roster, contract, trade, lockout, and free agency period is a legal and economic system built to control the business of play.

This episode traces how leagues, franchise owners, player unions, and collective bargaining agreements shape the modern sports machine, from salary caps and luxury taxes to free agency rules, tax aprons, amnesty clauses, and revenue sharing.

We look at how labor disputes in the NFL, NBA, and NHL reveal the hidden architecture of the industry, where lockouts become negotiating weapons and legal strategy becomes part of how leagues maintain competitive balance and franchise value.
