About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

After tracing the beginning of China’s Cultural Revolution in a previous episode, this deep dive steps backward to ask what came before it.

From the Opium Wars to the burning of the Old Summer Palace, the fall of imperial China, foreign occupation, civil war, and the long shadow of the “Century of Humiliation,” we trace the deeper history that shaped China before Mao and the Red Guards entered the frame.
