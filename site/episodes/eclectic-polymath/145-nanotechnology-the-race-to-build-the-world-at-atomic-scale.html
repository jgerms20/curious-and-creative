About this episode

AI is about gaining greater control over intelligence. Nanotechnology points toward something just as ambitious: gaining greater control over matter itself.

This episode looks at the path from the nanotechnology we already use to the far more radical possibility of atomically precise manufacturing. It gets into nanoparticles, molecular machines, top-down fabrication, chemical synthesis, positional mechanosynthesis, and the idea that future manufacturing systems might one day construct complex materials with near-atomic precision.

Key points

  • AI is about gaining greater control over intelligence.
  • Nanotechnology points toward something just as ambitious: gaining greater control over matter itself.
  • And we separate those challenges from more famous speculative fears like “gray goo.” This is a story about the engineering of matter, how nanotechnology differs from AI and quantum computing, and what happens if physical manufacturing eventually becomes as programmable as information.
