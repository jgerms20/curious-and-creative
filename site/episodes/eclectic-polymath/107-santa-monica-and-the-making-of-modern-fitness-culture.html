About this episode

Modern fitness culture didn't begin inside a luxury gym. It grew on the beach, in public, where bodies became performance, aspiration, entertainment, and eventually business.

This episode looks at how Santa Monica evolved from a coastal health destination into one of the birthplaces of modern fitness culture. From the original Muscle Beach and public acrobatics to pioneers like Abbye “Pudgy” Stockton and Jack LaLanne, it traces how physical culture became more professional, commercial, and visible.

We get into Hollywood’s influence, New Deal infrastructure, bodybuilding, television, boutique fitness, gentrification, and the growing obsession with optimization and longevity. This is a story about how one stretch of California coastline helped shape the way the world trains, looks, and thinks about the body.
