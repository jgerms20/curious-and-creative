About this episode

In 79 AD, Pompeii was a working Roman city of bakeries, bars, election posters, and graffiti — and almost nobody there knew the mountain behind it was a volcano. Latin didn't even have a word for one. Then, over about eighteen hours, the city stopped mid-sentence.

This episode tells it as a story: the ordinary day, the warnings nobody could read, Pliny watching from across the bay, the strange fact that most people actually escaped — and how a city gets forgotten for 1,500 years, then dug up to become the most famous disaster in the world. What we lost, what we learned, and why we can't stop looking.
