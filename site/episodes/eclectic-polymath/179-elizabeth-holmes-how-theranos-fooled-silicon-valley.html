About this episode

Theranos promised to reinvent medicine with a few drops of blood. Instead, it became one of Silicon Valley’s defining fraud stories.

This episode traces Elizabeth Holmes from ambitious young founder to the center of a company valued on technology that couldn’t perform as promised. We get into the powerful investors and board members surrounding Theranos, the machinery used to preserve its image, the treatment of employees who raised concerns, and the investigative work that finally cracked the story open.

But Theranos is bigger than one deceptive founder. It’s a story about what happens when charisma, elite networks, secrecy, and the mythology of the visionary entrepreneur outrun scientific proof. When medicine is involved, “fake it till you make it” stops being startup bravado and starts putting real people at risk.
