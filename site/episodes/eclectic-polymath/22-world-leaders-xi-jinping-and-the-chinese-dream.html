About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

Xi Jinping’s rise is a story about power built slowly, disciplined carefully, and then consolidated with historic force.

From his childhood inside China’s revolutionary elite to the trauma of the Cultural Revolution, his exile to rural Shaanxi, and his methodical climb through provincial politics, this episode traces how Xi became China’s most dominant leader since Mao Zedong.
