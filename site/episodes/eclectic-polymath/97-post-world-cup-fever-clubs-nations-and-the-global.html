About this episode

World football can look simple from the outside: countries, clubs, stars, tournaments, and fans. But underneath the game is a massive global operating system built from federations, leagues, clubs, owners, sponsors, broadcasters, academies, and international politics.

This episode looks at how football is structured across the world, from FIFA and continental confederations to domestic leagues, national teams, knockout cups, and elite competitions like the Champions League. It explores why club football and national team football operate by different rules, and how players move through overlapping calendars, contracts, identities, and loyalties.
