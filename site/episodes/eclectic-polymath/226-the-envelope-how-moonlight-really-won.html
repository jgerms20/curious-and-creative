About this episode

It is still the most shocking moment in Oscars history: La La Land announced as Best Picture, speeches underway — then the correction. Moonlight had won. This episode reconstructs that night minute by minute and asks the question underneath it: the count was never wrong, only the envelope was. So what actually failed?

Inside: the duplicate card that survived in the wrong set, the backstage photo tweeted minutes before the handoff, how preferential voting really works, why the results are memorized and written down nowhere, PwC's role since 1935 and the sealed envelope born in 1941, and the counterfactual the system cannot answer — if the count itself were ever wrong, no one would ever know.
