About this episode

A money-laundering investigation at a small car wash became one of the largest corruption scandals in Latin American history. What investigators uncovered inside Brazil’s political and corporate establishment would implicate powerful executives, major construction firms, and some of the country’s most influential political figures.

This episode traces the rise of Operation Car Wash, or Lava Jato, from its origins in 2014 to the sprawling bribery schemes involving Petrobras, engineering companies, political parties, and money brokers. It explores how the investigation expanded across borders, how Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva became one of its most consequential targets, and how the scandal reshaped Brazil’s political landscape.
