About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

This episode starts with a reread of The Three-Body Problem, probably my favorite fiction book of all time, and an opening scene that is hard to shake: a public denunciation, a crowd, and a teacher beaten to death.

That scene led to a bigger question: how does a society reach the point where violence like that becomes possible?
