About this episode

Spotify did not just change how people listen to music. It changed the entire economic structure around music, turning ownership into access, albums into streams, and discovery into an algorithmic experience.

This episode looks at Spotify’s rise from a Swedish startup fighting piracy to one of the most powerful audio platforms in the world. Built on peer-to-peer technology, clean user experience, and a legal alternative to downloading, Spotify helped pull the music industry into the streaming era while reshaping how artists, labels, and listeners interact.

We get into the company’s royalty model, its battles with major labels and tech rivals, the shift into podcasts, and the controversies around ghost artists, playlist power, and algorithms that can shape taste at scale. This is a story about the platform that helped save music revenue while permanently changing what music is worth.
