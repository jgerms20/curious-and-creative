About this episode

An Instagram reel argued Los Angeles is destined to become the greatest city in the world, and its comments kept returning to one phrase: the third wave. This episode audits the claim — the waves of LA's history, the city it never finished building, and the ocean it faces.

The Pacific passed the Atlantic in American trade in the early 1980s and now runs about two to one in goods. San Pedro Bay moves nearly a third of the nation's containerized trade. Mayors from Tom Bradley to Eric Garcetti have called LA the capital of the Pacific Rim. The numbers, the rivals, and the counterarguments — peak China, the Taiwan Strait, a county population that has started to shrink — all get their hearing.

Plus: the streetcar city LA built and tore out, the subway it skipped until 1993, why the haven case survives only with its costs priced in, and what the next 50, 100, and 200 years can honestly claim.
