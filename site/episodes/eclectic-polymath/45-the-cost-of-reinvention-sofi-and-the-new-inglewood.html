About this episode

SoFi Stadium did not just bring football back to Los Angeles. It reshaped the future of Inglewood, turning a historically Black working and middle class city into one of the most visible sports and entertainment districts in the country.

This episode traces how Inglewood moved from financial distress, high unemployment, and municipal pressure into a new era of private investment, stadium development, rising property values, and global attention.

We look at the promises and tradeoffs behind the transformation, from civic revenue and event day opportunity to rent pressure, displacement, lawsuits, infrastructure costs, and the decline of long term Black residency.
