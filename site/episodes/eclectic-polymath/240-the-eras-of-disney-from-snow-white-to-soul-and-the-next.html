About this episode

Ask a room of Disney fans to name the eras and you will start an argument — because every border in Disney's century is a disguised theory of what an era even is. Does the Silver Age end in 1959, when the fairy-tale style peaked, or in 1967, when Walt died? This episode maps the whole taxonomy: the Golden Age, the wartime package years, the Bronze Age drift, the Dark Age, the Renaissance, the Revival, and the era we are in now that still has no name.

The anchors are two films. The Lion King, the Renaissance's commercial peak — made by the crew the studio itself called the B-team while its A-listers worked on Pocahontas. And Soul, Pixar's creative landmark and the purest symbol of the streaming era: an Oscar winner sent straight to Disney+ at Christmas, free, while the theatrical machine idled.
