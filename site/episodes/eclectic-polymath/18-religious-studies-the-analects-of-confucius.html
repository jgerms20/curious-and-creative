About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

The Analects of Confucius brings us into a tradition where the sacred is found less in divine revelation and more in the everyday discipline of becoming a better person.

Collected through the memories of disciples and organized across generations, the text preserves Confucius’s teachings on ritual, virtue, education, leadership, family, and the moral habits that hold a society together.
