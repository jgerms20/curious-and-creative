About this episode

Xbox has spent years acquiring studios, expanding Game Pass, and building one of the largest portfolios in gaming. But owning more doesn’t automatically mean running a better business.

This episode looks at Asha Sharma’s approach to reshaping Xbox around a more disciplined operating model. We get into her background outside traditional gaming, the influence of companies like Meta and Instacart, and what happens when a leader trained around data, margins, and operational efficiency takes control of a creative industry built on expensive bets, long development cycles, and unpredictable hits.
