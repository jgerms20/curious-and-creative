About this episode

The Nintendo Wii was not the most powerful console of its generation, and that was exactly the point. Instead of competing on graphics, specs, and hardcore gaming prestige, Nintendo built a system around motion, simplicity, accessibility, and the radical idea that almost anyone could play.

This episode looks at how the Wii became one of the most important cultural and business disruptions in video game history. Through its Blue Ocean Strategy, intuitive motion controls, family-friendly design, and breakout titles like Wii Sports, Nintendo expanded gaming beyond traditional audiences and turned living rooms, retirement homes, parties, and family gatherings into play spaces.
