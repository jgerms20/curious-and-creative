About this episode

In 2011, Charlie Sheen — the highest-paid actor on television — went on live TV and declared he had "tiger blood" and "Adonis DNA," that he was "winning." America laughed, then watched it curdle in real time. But what was actually going on? This is the full story: the Malibu kid who became a movie star in Platoon and Wall Street, the $1.8 million-an-episode king of Two and a Half Men, and the unraveling — the rehabs, the Plaza Hotel, the firing, the $100 million lawsuit, the disastrous Detroit tour, and the media machine that turned a man's breakdown into content. We separate the documented facts from the lore, trace the money from peak to collapse, and follow Sheen through the 2015 HIV disclosure, sobriety, the 2025 memoir and Netflix documentary, and his unlikely 2026 return to television alongside his father.
