About this episode

We’re doing something a little different. Instead of the usual Eclectic Polymath deep dive, this episode shares a conversation I attended with Kamasi Washington at the USC Thornton School of Music. Held September 17, 2026 at Newman Recital Hall, “Beyond Category” featured Washington in conversation with USC Thornton Dean Jason King about music, imagination, community, and what happens when artists stop letting genre define them.

The conversation moves through Washington’s career from The Epic and Harmony of Difference to his collaborations with Kendrick Lamar, his South LA musical community, Bruce Lee, composing across mediums, and the importance of experiencing music together in physical space. He also looks back on appearing at USC a decade earlier and reflects on how his ambition has shifted from chasing specific goals toward what he calls searching for something more infinite.
