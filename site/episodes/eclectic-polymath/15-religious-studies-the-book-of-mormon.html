About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

The Book of Mormon enters religious history as a modern scripture with ancient claims, emerging out of nineteenth century America and reshaping the boundaries of Christian imagination.

Through Joseph Smith, golden plates, prophecy, migration, restoration, and the rise of the Latter day Saint movement, this text became the foundation for one of the most distinctive religious traditions born in the United States.
