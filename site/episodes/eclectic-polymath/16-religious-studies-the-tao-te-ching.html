About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

The Tao Te Ching is short, quiet, and deceptively simple, but its ideas have carried across centuries of Chinese philosophy, religion, politics, and spiritual practice.

At its center is the Tao, the Way, a reality that cannot be fully named, controlled, or forced. Through paradox, softness, humility, nature, and non action, the text offers a vision of power that moves differently from domination.
