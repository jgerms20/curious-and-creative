About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

This episode starts with Kevin Hart, and a bigger question: what happens when hustle becomes a business model and celebrity becomes infrastructure?

From his early days in Philadelphia as Lil Kev to stand up, movies, tours, Hartbeat, private equity, brand partnerships, and venture deals, we trace how Kevin Hart turned fame into structure, and structure into ownership.
