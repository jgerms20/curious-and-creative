About this episode

In his new memoir, Ari Emanuel tells the story of how Jeffrey Katzenberg got pushed out of Disney — and what got built next. In October 1994, Katzenberg, Steven Spielberg, and David Geffen announced DreamWorks SKG: the first new major studio in decades, priced at $2.7 billion before it had made a single film, on three names and one grudge.

Key points

  • the Disney Renaissance Katzenberg ran and the presidency he was denied when Frank Wells died
  • the 2-percent lawsuit and the settlement Geffen brokered at his Malibu home
  • Paul Allen's $500 million and Miky Lee's $300 million for Korea's CJ
  • the Oscar run, Shrek, the Sinbad collapse, and the company sold off in pieces.
  • Steve Jobs, who bought Pixar for $10 million, funded its losses for a decade, and built the opposite of DreamWorks in every particular
