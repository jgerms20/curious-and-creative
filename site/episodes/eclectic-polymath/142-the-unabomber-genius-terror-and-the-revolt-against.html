About this episode

Ted Kaczynski, aka "The Unabomber", was a gifted mathematician who became a domestic terrorist—and decades later, arguments from his manifesto still surface whenever people fear technology is becoming too powerful.

This episode traces Kaczynski from his unusual academic rise through Harvard, mathematics, Berkeley, and his retreat to a cabin in Montana. It examines his growing hostility toward industrial society, the bombing campaign that killed and injured innocent people, the massive investigation that followed, and the manifesto that ultimately helped authorities identify him.

  • Some of those concerns now echo through debates about algorithms, AI, automation, and surveillance—but that doesn’t separate the ideas from the violence he chose to commit.
