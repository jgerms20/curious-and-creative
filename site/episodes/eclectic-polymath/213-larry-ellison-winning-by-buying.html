#213 Larry Ellison: Winning by Buying

About this episode

For one day in September 2025, Larry Ellison was the richest man alive. He was 81, and the money did not come from a new invention. It came from forty years of databases, data centers, and a doctrine he never apologized for: if you cannot beat them, buy them.

Ellison started with a borrowed name and a CIA codename. Oracle was a covert project before it was a company, and the company nearly died in 1990 before the acquisition spree that swallowed PeopleSoft, Sun, and half the enterprise software world. He won the America's Cup from 8-1 down because quitting was not in the operating system. He bought 98 percent of a Hawaiian island. And in his eighties, the AI boom made his infrastructure the toll road everyone else has to drive on, all the way to custody of TikTok's American data.

This is the foundation story. The next episode is his son, who just bought Hollywood.
