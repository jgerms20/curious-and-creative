About this episode

Peptides sit at the center of one of the biggest health stories of the moment, but their history stretches far beyond today’s weight loss boom.

This episode traces the peptide revolution from the discovery of insulin in 1921 to the rise of GLP 1 drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy, showing how short chains of amino acids became some of the most powerful biological messengers in modern medicine.

We look at how peptides bridge the world between small molecule drugs and large biologics, with major breakthroughs in obesity, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, skincare, recovery, and metabolic health.
