About this episode

Degrassi was not just a teen show. It was a cultural testing ground for what youth television was allowed to talk about, who it was allowed to represent, and how honest a show for young people could be.

This episode looks at how the Canadian franchise pushed television into uncomfortable but necessary territory, taking on abortion, sexuality, school violence, identity, race, class, mental health, disability, and LGBTQ+ representation before many American teen shows were willing to go there.

We get into why Canada mattered to the Degrassi formula: the multicultural setting, the less glossy production style, the public-service spirit, and a cultural environment that gave the show room to be more direct than the advertiser-driven world of American network television.
