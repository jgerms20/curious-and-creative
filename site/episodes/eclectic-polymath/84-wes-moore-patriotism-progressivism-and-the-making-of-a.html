About this episode

Wes Moore’s rise is not just a story about biography. It is a story about how personal narrative becomes political capital — and what happens when a life built around service, ambition, patriotism, and moral purpose enters the machinery of executive power.

This episode looks at Moore’s journey from Rhodes Scholar, combat veteran, author, nonprofit leader, and CEO to governor of Maryland. His political identity blends inclusive competitiveness, public service, corporate pragmatism, and a carefully constructed story of resilience that helped make him one of the most closely watched Democratic figures in the country.
