About this episode

Bob Iger didn’t build Disney from the ground up. He figured out how to make an already powerful company dramatically bigger without losing the creative engines that made it valuable.

This episode traces Iger’s rise from an entry-level production role to the top of The Walt Disney Company. It gets into his acquisition strategy around Pixar, Marvel, Lucasfilm, and 21st Century Fox, and how he balanced centralized ownership with enough creative autonomy for those brands to keep feeling distinct.

We also get into Disney+, corporate politics, crisis management, CEO succession, and Iger’s reputation as a calm, diplomatic operator in an industry built on ego and high-stakes bets. This is a story about scale, storytelling, and how power gets built by acquiring the right things—and then knowing when not to interfere.
