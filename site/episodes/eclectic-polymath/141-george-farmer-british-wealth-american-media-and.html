About this episode

George Farmer sits at the intersection of British wealth, American conservative media, technology, and politics—but his path to influence began long before most people knew his name.

This episode traces Farmer’s background through his father, Lord Michael Farmer, and the financial and political world he grew up around before following his own move into conservative activism and technology. It gets into inherited access, Oxford, Turning Point UK, Parler, GB News, and the distinction between family wealth and the influence Farmer built for himself.

We also get into his marriage to Candace Owens, his traditionalist Catholic faith, and the broader network connecting British conservatism with American political media and digital platforms. This is a story about money, ideology, marriage, and how modern influence moves across borders through families, institutions, and media.
