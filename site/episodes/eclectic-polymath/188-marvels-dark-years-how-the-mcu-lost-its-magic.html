About this episode

For more than a decade, Marvel Studios seemed unstoppable. What began as an ambitious experiment in interconnected storytelling became Hollywood's most successful franchise, culminating in Avengers: Endgame. Then something changed. The movies kept coming, the universe kept expanding, but the magic became harder to sustain.

This episode traces Marvel's rise under Kevin Feige, the creative decisions that made its early success possible, and the pressures that followed. We explore Disney's streaming ambitions, the explosion of Disney+ series, production challenges, and why genuinely inventive projects like WandaVision could coexist with increasingly inconsistent films and television.
