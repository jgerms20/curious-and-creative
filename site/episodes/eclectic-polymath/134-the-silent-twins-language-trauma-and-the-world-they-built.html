About this episode

June and Jennifer Gibbons became famous as “The Silent Twins,” but their silence was only the most visible part of a much more complicated story.

This episode traces their childhood, racial isolation in Wales, selective mutism, and the intensely private world they built together through language, ritual, imagination, and writing. It also looks at the way their behavior was pathologized, how institutions interpreted their bond, and why their literary output reveals a far richer interior life than the public mythology ever allowed.
