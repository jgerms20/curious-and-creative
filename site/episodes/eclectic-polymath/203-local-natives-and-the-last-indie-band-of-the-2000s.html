About this episode

Local Natives were five kids from Orange County who moved into a Silver Lake house together, named their debut album after it — Gorilla Manor — and became one of the defining bands of the 2000s indie era. This episode tells their story as the door into that whole world: the blogs that could make a band overnight, Pitchfork as kingmaker, Spaceland and the Echo, and a sound built on stacked harmonies and restless percussion.

Then the era turns: Hummingbird, made with Aaron Dessner in the shadow of grief; streaming playlists replacing the blogs; Silver Lake pricing out its own scene; and the band's long arc through lineup changes to where they stand today — and what the indie era permanently changed about how music gets made, sold, and heard.
