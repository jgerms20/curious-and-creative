About this episode

Anime is everywhere now—but the style, industry, and storytelling language behind it were shaped by a very specific history.

This episode traces anime from early Japanese animation and postwar production constraints through the influence of Osamu Tezuka, television, serialization, and the rise of studios like Studio Ghibli. We get into how limited budgets helped create a distinct visual grammar, why anime developed differently from Western animation, and how those choices opened the door to more mature themes, longer stories, and entire genres that could grow with their audiences.
