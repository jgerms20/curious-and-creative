About this episode

Five days before Israel votes, the man leading half the polls is a general who has never held office — and the math says nobody can win. This episode reconstructs Gadi Eisenkot's rise, the party he built in a year, and the deadlock waiting on the other side of October 27.

Inside: the son he buried and the doctrine he wrote; Gantz's exit and the threshold arithmetic behind it; the red line he read into the record ("I will not serve under Netanyahu"); the state-inquiry fight driving the whole election; and the honest counter-case — the ten-poll average actually has Likud ahead, and Israel's generals have a mixed record once they take the chair.
