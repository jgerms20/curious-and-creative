About this episode

Sundar Pichai didn’t build Google from scratch. His challenge was arguably harder: inherit one of the most powerful companies in the world and figure out how to keep it dominant as technology changed around it.

This episode traces Pichai’s path from a middle-class upbringing in India through IIT Kharagpur, Stanford, and Wharton to his rise inside Google. It looks at the bets that defined his ascent, including Chrome, Android, search distribution, and the steady expansion of Google into a global technology infrastructure company.

We get into his calm management style, the transition from founder-led Google to institutional Alphabet, antitrust pressure, internal culture, and the generative AI race now forcing the company to evolve again. This is a story about stewardship, scale, and what it takes to reinvent an empire without breaking the systems that made it powerful.
