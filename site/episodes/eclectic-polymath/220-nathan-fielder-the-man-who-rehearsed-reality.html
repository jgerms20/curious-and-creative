About this episode

He has a business degree, a pilot’s license, and a theory about why people don’t speak up.

Nathan Fielder turned a small-business advice show into a series of experiments on how people comply, perform, and go along: a coffee shop that ran on parody law, a jacket company built on a joke that stopped being one, and a finale that quietly became the best film of its year. Then The Rehearsal scaled the method up, simulations inside simulations, until a season of television about cockpit communication ended with him at the controls of a real 737 full of actors, arguing with the FAA on CNN. This episode is about the lens itself: how the deadpan, the paperwork, and the escalating commitment produce truths no interview could, and the argument that has followed him the whole way, empathy machine or cruelty machine.
