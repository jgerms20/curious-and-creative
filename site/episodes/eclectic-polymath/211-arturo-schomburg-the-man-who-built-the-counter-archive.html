About this episode

Arturo Schomburg was a kid in San Juan when a teacher told him Black people had no history. He spent the rest of his life proving her wrong, one book at a time.

This episode follows the Afro-Puerto Rican collector who built the archive Black history runs on, and widens into the bigger question his life poses: who decides what gets saved? Slavery's records were written by enslavers, so Schomburg built a counter-archive inside the public library — and a century later we're still working out what that means.
