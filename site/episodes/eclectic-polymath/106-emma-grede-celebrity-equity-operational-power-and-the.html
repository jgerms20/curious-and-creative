About this episode

Emma Grede built her career by understanding that celebrity attention is valuable—but only if someone can turn it into a durable business.

This episode traces her rise from a working-class upbringing in East London and a background in fashion production to becoming the strategic force behind brands like Good American and SKIMS. It looks at how she helped industrialize the celebrity-brand model through product development, supply chains, retail distribution, operational discipline, and equity ownership.

We get into her partnerships, her approach to scale, the Fifteen Percent Pledge, and the debate around her demanding views on work and office culture. This is a story about the person behind the person—the operator who turns cultural relevance into infrastructure, ownership, and long-term value.
