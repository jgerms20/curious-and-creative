About this episode

Josh Kushner built his career in the shadow of one of America’s most visible families, but his real power came from becoming unusually good at staying out of the spotlight.

This episode traces Kushner’s path from a real-estate dynasty into venture capital, where Thrive Capital became one of the most influential investors in technology. It gets into his concentrated-bet strategy, early stakes in companies like OpenAI, Instagram, and Stripe, and the way Thrive built deep relationships with founders instead of spreading capital across hundreds of companies.

We also get into family power, discretion, the OpenAI leadership crisis, and Kushner’s expansion beyond software into major cultural assets like the Los Angeles Lakers. This is a story about a quieter form of influence—how capital, relationships, and patience can create enormous power without requiring constant public visibility.
