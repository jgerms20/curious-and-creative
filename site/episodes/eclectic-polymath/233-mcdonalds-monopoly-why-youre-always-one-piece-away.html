About this episode

You have never won McDonald's Monopoly, and neither has anyone you know — but you have absolutely been one piece away. This episode opens the machine up: who invented the promotion in 1987, how the odds are actually printed, why the law requires a mail-in route that gives you the same chance for the price of two stamps, and the twelve years when the winning pieces were being stolen from the inside.

Inside: the rare-piece design (in the 2014 rules, Boardwalk was a single piece at 1 in 651,841,628); the psychology of the near-miss; Jerome Jacobson, the security chief who ran the theft ring until the FBI's Operation Final Answer took down 51 people in August 2001 — weeks before 9/11 buried the story; and the 2026 comeback running right now, where the million dollars no longer exists as a physical piece at all.
