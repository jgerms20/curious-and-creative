About this episode

Bill Simmons started as a sports-obsessed internet writer with a personal voice, a sharp sense of fandom, and a feel for where media was headed before the industry fully understood it.

This episode traces Simmons from his early “Sports Guy” days to his rise inside ESPN, where he helped push the company into blogs, podcasts, documentaries, longform writing, and personality-driven digital media through projects like Page 2, 30 for 30, and Grantland.

We get into the friction that eventually broke the relationship apart: Simmons’s public criticism of the NFL, his clashes with ESPN’s corporate structure, and the tension between a creator’s personal brand and a legacy institution built around control.
