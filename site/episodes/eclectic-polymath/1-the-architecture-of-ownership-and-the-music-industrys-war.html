About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

This episode starts with a simple but loaded question: what does it actually mean for an artist to own their work?

From there, we get into the long fight over masters, publishing, contracts, and creative control in the music industry. We look at how the business was built, why ownership became such a defining battle, and how artists like Michael Jackson, Prince, and Kanye West each challenged the system in their own way.
