About this episode

Eczema can look like a problem on the surface of the skin, but the biology underneath it is much more complicated.

This episode looks at atopic dermatitis as a disease of both the skin barrier and the immune system. It gets into genetics, filaggrin, inflammatory signaling, environmental triggers, and why eczema overlaps with conditions like asthma and allergies. It also traces how treatment evolved from broad approaches like topical steroids toward therapies designed to interrupt specific immune pathways.

We get into biologics, JAK inhibitors, the growing promise of precision medicine, and emerging research around the microbiome, AI, and long-term disease modification. This is a story about how a common and often misunderstood skin condition became a window into the rapidly changing science of inflammation.
