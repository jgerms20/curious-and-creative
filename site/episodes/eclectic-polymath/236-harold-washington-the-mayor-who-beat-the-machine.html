About this episode

Harold Washington didn't inherit Chicago. He registered it. Before the 1983 run he set a condition: 50,000 new Black voters, or no campaign. Black Chicago delivered nearly double, and the machine that had run the city for half a century met the one candidate it couldn't absorb.

This episode goes deep on the whole arc: the Daley machine and the Black submachine Washington came up under, the ugliest general election in modern Chicago history, and the Council Wars that froze his first term. Then the record itself: the appointments, Executive Order 85-2 opening city contracts, Fair Share budgeting, and what it concretely meant for Black Chicago.
