About this episode

The 2002 Spider-Man movie didn’t just launch a successful superhero franchise. It helped prove that comic-book characters could become the foundation of a new global entertainment economy.

This episode looks at how Spider-Man built on the groundwork laid by films like Batman and X-Men, then pushed the model much further. Its box-office success, marketing power, licensing reach, and global appeal showed studios that intellectual property could drive not just one hit movie, but an entire system of sequels, merchandise, partnerships, and long-term audience loyalty.
