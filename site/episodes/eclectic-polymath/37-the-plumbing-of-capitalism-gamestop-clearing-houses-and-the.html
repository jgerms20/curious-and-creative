About this episode

Every stock trade looks simple on the surface, but behind the buy button is a hidden system built to move money, manage risk, and keep markets from collapsing under their own speed.

This episode traces the role of clearing houses, especially the DTCC, as the invisible financial infrastructure that guarantees trades, demands collateral, manages settlement, and quietly decides how much risk the system can absorb.

We look at how this machinery became impossible to ignore during the 2021 GameStop and Robinhood trading freeze, when margin requirements, retail traders, broker limits, and opaque market plumbing suddenly became part of the public conversation.
