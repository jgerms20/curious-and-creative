About this episode

Alice Coltrane is often introduced through her connection to John Coltrane, but her story is much bigger than widowhood. She was a visionary musician, composer, spiritual seeker, and architect of a sound world that turned grief, devotion, and discipline into something transcendent.

This episode looks at her evolution from a classically trained pianist in Detroit to one of the defining figures of spiritual jazz. Through harp, organ, synthesizer, orchestration, and devotional practice, Alice Coltrane built music that blended Black gospel, avant-garde jazz, Vedic chants, Eastern spirituality, and her own search for healing.
