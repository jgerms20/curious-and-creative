About this episode

Canada is often framed as polite, stable, and secondary on the world stage, but that image can obscure something much bigger: a country with enormous natural wealth, strategic resources, global financial reach, and quiet geopolitical leverage.

This episode looks at Canada as more than a middle power, tracing how its sovereignty, resource economy, mining sector, freshwater, energy reserves, and pension funds give it influence far beyond its soft-spoken reputation. It also explores the tensions underneath that power, from productivity struggles and housing instability to imperial legal foundations and unresolved Indigenous land claims.
