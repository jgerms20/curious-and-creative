About this episode

Mongolia is the emptiest country on Earth — about two people per square kilometer — and a third of it still moves with the seasons. It's also the homeland of the largest land empire in history, now sitting quietly between Russia and China.

This episode traces how that happens: nomadism as a technology, not a rootlessness; Chinggis Khan and the empire that fragmented into other people's countries; Buddhism, Soviet decades, and a revolution that killed no one; and the modern squeeze of selling nearly everything to one neighbor while buying fuel from the other. How does a small country between two giants stay itself?
