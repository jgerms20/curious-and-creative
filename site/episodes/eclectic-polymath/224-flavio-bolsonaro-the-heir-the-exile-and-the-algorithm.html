About this episode

On October 4, Flávio Bolsonaro beat Lula in the first round of Brazil's presidential election, 56.1 million votes to 53.9 million. His father, the man who built the movement, followed it under house arrest: 27 years for the coup plot, barred from office until 2030, banned from even posting online.

So how is the movement winning? This episode maps the succession: the heir chosen from custody, the brother running foreign policy from exile in Texas, the stepmother and brothers winning Senate seats of their own, the biggest party in Congress, a billion reais a year in public money, and a runoff being fought inside Instagram's algorithm, with Brazil's electoral court fining Meta into neutrality.

The runoff is October 25. Win or lose, Bolsonarismo has already stopped being a man and become a family business. Here is how the franchise works.
