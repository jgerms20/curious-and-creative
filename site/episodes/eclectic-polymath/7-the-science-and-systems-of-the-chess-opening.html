About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

This episode starts with a question every developing chess player eventually runs into: how do you stop just moving pieces and start building a real position?

From the Romantic era of daring sacrifices to the modern computer age of Stockfish and AlphaZero, we trace how chess openings evolved from instinct and style into a deeper science of time, space, structure, and control.
