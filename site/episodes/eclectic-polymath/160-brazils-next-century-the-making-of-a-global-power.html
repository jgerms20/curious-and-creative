About this episode

Brazil has almost everything a country could want: continental scale, enormous agricultural capacity, energy resources, freshwater, biodiversity, major cities, a huge internal market, and an increasingly sophisticated digital economy. Yet for generations, the country has repeatedly seemed to approach its potential without fully realizing it.

This episode looks beyond the Brazil of today to ask what the country could become by 2100. We explore an aging population, productivity and education, infrastructure, tax reform, digital systems like Pix and Drex, industrial development, food and energy, and the difficult balance between exploiting Brazil’s extraordinary natural resources and preserving the environmental wealth that makes the country globally important.

Key points

  • We examine the structural bottlenecks that have repeatedly constrained Brazilian development—and the economic, technological, demographic, and geopolitical forces that could make this century different.
