About this episode

For fifty years, the American smoke shop sold millions of dollars of cannabis gear under one rule: nobody could say what any of it was for. This episode traces the head shop from its 1966 counterculture birth — the Psychedelic Shop on Haight Street — through the "for tobacco use only" era, Operation Pipe Dreams, and Tommy Chong's prison sentence.

Then the turn: how the AIDS crisis, Dennis Peron, and Proposition 215 transformed the gray-zone storefront into the legal dispensary — and how MedMen's billion-dollar "Apple Store of Weed," a punishing federal tax rule, and LA's broken licensing system explain why the original head shop is still standing.
