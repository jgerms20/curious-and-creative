About this episode

Two people can love each other for years without the government caring very much. Then they say "I do," sign a document, and suddenly courts, tax authorities, insurers, employers, immigration officials, and inheritance law all have something to say about their relationship. So what actually happens when love becomes marriage?

This episode traces marriage from an institution built around kinship, property, inheritance, and family alliances into the modern love story—and follows the wedding industry that grew around it. We get into diamond rings, white dresses, registries, bachelor parties, the so-called "wedding tax," destination weddings, and why a celebration that can legally happen at a courthouse for very little money can also become a five- or six-figure production.
