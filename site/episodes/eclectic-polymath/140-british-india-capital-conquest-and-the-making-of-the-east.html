About this episode

Britain’s empire in India didn’t begin with the British government. It began with a corporation.

This episode traces how the East India Company evolved from a trading enterprise into a military and political power capable of governing enormous territories. It gets into the decline of the Mughal Empire, alliances with Indian rulers, taxation, colonial extraction, deindustrialization, and the strange reality of a private company gradually acquiring the machinery of a state.

We also get into the rebellion of 1857, the transition to direct Crown rule, the British Raj, railways and bureaucracy, racial hierarchy, Indian nationalism, and the violent Partition of 1947. This is a story about how commerce became conquest—and how one of history’s largest empires grew from the intersection of capital, military power, and colonial rule.
