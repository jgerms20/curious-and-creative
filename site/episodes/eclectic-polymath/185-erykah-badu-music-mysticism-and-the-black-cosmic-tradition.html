About this episode

Erykah Badu has never really fit inside “neo-soul.” From the beginning, her music has pulled together spirituality, Black history, futurism, fashion, humor, wellness, and a fiercely self-defined sense of identity.

This episode traces Badu from her South Dallas upbringing and the arrival of Baduizm through the increasingly experimental world she built around herself. We explore the influences behind that world—from the Five Percent Nation and Kemetic imagery to West African traditions—and how those ideas became part of her music, performances, visual language, and public persona.
