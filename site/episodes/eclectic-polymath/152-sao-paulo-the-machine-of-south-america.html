About this episode

São Paulo transformed from an isolated hilltop colonial mission into the undeniable economic engine of South America. Driven by the 19th-century coffee boom and breakthrough rail corridors, the city exploded into a global powerhouse powered by relentless ambition and waves of global migration.

This episode unpacks how coffee wealth, immigrant labor, and modernist architecture built a financial giant—and how the city navigates the massive scale and stark inequalities of being South America's dominant metropolis.
