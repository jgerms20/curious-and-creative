About this episode

Love often gets treated like a mystery, a feeling, or a force that either exists or disappears. Robert Sternberg’s Triangular Theory of Love offers a different way to see it: as a structure built from intimacy, passion, and commitment.

This episode looks at how those three elements combine to create different forms of love, from infatuation and friendship to empty love, romantic love, companionate love, and consummate love. Instead of treating relationships as purely emotional, Sternberg’s framework makes love feel architectural — something with shape, balance, pressure points, and missing pieces.
