About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

After tracing Motown, artist ownership, and the modern music machine, this episode moves into the next frontier: what happens when the asset is no longer just the song, but the artist’s voice, style, likeness, and data?

From AI licensing deals to voice cloning, digital replicas, viral deepfakes, and laws like the ELVIS Act, we trace how the music industry is trying to define a new rights stack for the AI era.
