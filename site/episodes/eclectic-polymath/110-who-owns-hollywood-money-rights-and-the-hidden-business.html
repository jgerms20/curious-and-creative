About this episode

A movie can make a billion dollars and still somehow be called “unprofitable.”

This episode looks at the financial and legal machinery behind Hollywood, from how films get financed to who actually owns the rights once they’re made. It gets into production companies, studios, talent agencies, intellectual property, revenue waterfalls, and the infamous world of “Hollywood accounting,” where box office success and actual profit can be two very different things.

We also get into why ownership matters more than a big upfront paycheck, how filmmakers like Christopher Nolan and Ryan Coogler have pushed for greater creative control, and why the most valuable asset in entertainment is often the underlying rights themselves. This is a story about the business beneath the glamour—and who really holds the power when the credits roll.
