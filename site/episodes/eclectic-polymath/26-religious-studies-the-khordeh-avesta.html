About this episode

Welcome back to The Eclectic Polymath Podcast, a curiosity driven deep dive show built around the questions that pull us in.

The Khordeh Avesta brings us into the daily prayer life of Zoroastrianism, one of the world’s oldest religious traditions and a faith built around truth, purity, sacred words, and devotion to Ahura Mazda.

This episode moves through liturgical hymns, Nyāyeshes, Yashts, purification rites, prayers for different times of day, and the belief that holy speech carries spiritual power.
