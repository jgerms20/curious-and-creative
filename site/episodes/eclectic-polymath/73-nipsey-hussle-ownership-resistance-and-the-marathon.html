About this episode

Nipsey Hussle was more than a rapper with an inspiring slogan. He was building a philosophy of ownership, self-sufficiency, technology, and neighborhood power rooted directly in South Los Angeles.

This episode looks at Nipsey’s evolution from street life and music into community activism, business strategy, and localized economic development. His “Marathon” mindset was not just about persistence. It was a blueprint for reinvesting in the block, resisting displacement, preserving community assets, and imagining Black wealth as something built collectively over time.
