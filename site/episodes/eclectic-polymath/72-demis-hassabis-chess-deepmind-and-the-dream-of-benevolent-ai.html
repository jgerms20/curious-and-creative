About this episode

Demis Hassabis did not arrive at artificial intelligence from Silicon Valley hype alone. His path runs through chess, video games, neuroscience, and a lifelong fascination with how intelligence works — first in the human mind, then inside machines.

This episode looks at Hassabis’s evolution from child chess prodigy and game designer to co-founder of DeepMind, one of the most important AI labs in the world. Through milestones like AlphaGo and AlphaFold, the story traces how DeepMind turned abstract questions about learning, strategy, and biology into breakthroughs with global scientific and technological consequences.
