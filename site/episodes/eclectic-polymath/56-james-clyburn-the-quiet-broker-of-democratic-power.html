About this episode

James E. Clyburn is not the loudest figure in American politics, but few people have shaped Democratic power more quietly, more patiently, or more effectively.

This episode looks at Clyburn as a master of political infrastructure: a South Carolina power broker whose influence was built through Black churches, HBCUs, local officials, civil rights networks, and the kind of institutional trust that does not always show up on cable news.

We trace how that influence reached its defining national moment in the 2020 Democratic primary, when Clyburn’s endorsement helped revive Joe Biden’s campaign and reset the direction of the presidential race.
