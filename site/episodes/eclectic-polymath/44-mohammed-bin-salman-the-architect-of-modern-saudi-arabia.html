About this episode

Mohammed bin Salman is one of the most powerful and controversial leaders in the world, but his story begins inside the Saudi royal family, where inheritance, loyalty, ambition, and proximity to power shape who gets to rule.

This episode traces MBS from his upbringing as the son of King Salman to his rapid rise through the Saudi state, his consolidation of power, and his emergence as the face of a younger, more aggressive, more centralized Saudi Arabia.

We look at how he used Vision 2030, the Public Investment Fund, NEOM, entertainment reforms, women’s rights changes, and global investments to present himself as the architect of a new Saudi future.
