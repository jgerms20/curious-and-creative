About this episode

Medical tourism used to sound like a luxury niche, but for many people it has become something much more urgent: a workaround for healthcare systems that are too expensive, too slow, or too difficult to navigate at home.

This episode looks at the global medical tourism economy, from dental work and cosmetic surgery to fertility care, pharmaceuticals, and life-saving procedures. As patients travel to places like Mexico, Thailand, Turkey, and beyond, healthcare becomes a cross-border marketplace shaped by cost, access, accreditation, government investment, and trust.
