About this episode

Los Angeles erupted twice within a generation. The Watts Rebellion of 1965 and the 1992 Los Angeles uprising began with different immediate events, but both emerged from much deeper tensions around policing, economic exclusion, housing, and the geography of race in the city.

This episode connects those moments to the Los Angeles that existed underneath them: racially restrictive housing, segregated neighborhoods, disappearing economic opportunity, and decades of conflict between law enforcement and Black communities. We move from Watts in 1965 to the killing of Latasha Harlins, the videotaped beating of Rodney King, the acquittal of four LAPD officers, and the unrest that spread across Los Angeles in 1992.
