About this episode

If Gen Z and Gen Alpha humor sometimes feels completely incomprehensible, that may actually be the point.

This episode looks at how younger audiences use absurdity, irony, chaos, memes, and layered internet references to create a comedic language that often works less like a punchline and more like a shared social code. Drawing on new research into Gen Z and Gen Alpha humor, it explores how comedy has become a way to process anxiety, signal belonging, and make sense of an increasingly strange world.

We get into meme culture, participatory jokes, platform-native humor, local versus global references, and why brands so often fail when they try to imitate the language instead of understanding the community behind it. This is a story about how humor evolves with technology—and why the funniest thing online is often funny precisely because not everyone gets it.
