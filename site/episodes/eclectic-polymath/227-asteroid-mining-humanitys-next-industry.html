About this episode

What is actually out there, and what could it mean for us if we could reach it? Asteroids are failed planets, frozen feedstock from the solar system's construction, and water locked in clay older than Earth. This episode explores what asteroid mining really is: the metal cores of shattered worlds, the platinum-group metals the hydrogen economy is running short on, and the gas stations floating at the top of Earth's gravity well.

Along the way: the missions that already went out and touched them (Hayabusa2, OSIRIS-REx, NASA's Psyche, China's Tianwen-2), the startups that tried and died, how close the technology honestly is, the price paradox that breaks the famous business model, who owns an asteroid's water, and the one demonstration that would turn a prospectus into an industry.
