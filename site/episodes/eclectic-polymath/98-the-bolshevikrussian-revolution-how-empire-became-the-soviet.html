About this episode

The Soviet Union did not appear out of nowhere. It emerged from the collapse of an empire already weakened by poverty, autocracy, war, industrial strain, political repression, and the inability of the Romanov dynasty to adapt to a changing world.

This episode looks at how the Russian Empire unraveled and how revolutionary forces transformed that collapse into the Soviet state. From agrarian inequality and World War I to the fall of the Tsar, Dual Power, Bolshevik strategy, Menshevik conflict, and Lenin’s seizure of the moment in 1917, the story follows one of the most consequential political ruptures in modern history.
