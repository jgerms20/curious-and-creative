About this episode

For two decades, Jeffrey Epstein's operation ran through two professionals: his lawyer Darren Indyke and his accountant Richard Kahn. On October 21, 2026, New Mexico's attorney general sued them — the first law-enforcement action either man has ever faced. This episode asks who they are, what the documented record actually shows, and why accountability arrived as a civil suit seven years after Epstein's death.

Inside: the 1953 Trust in Epstein's own words (the $50 million and $25 million bequests, the forgiven debts); the estate's full arithmetic, from $577 million to the $121 million paid to 136 claimants; their sworn congressional denials; the banks that paid hundreds of millions while no individual was charged; and what the 49-page complaint alleges — flagged as allegation throughout, with their denials on the record.
