About this episode

The Guru Granth Sahib sits at the center of Sikhism as scripture, song, spiritual guide, and living authority.

Opening with the Mool Mantar and moving through hymns, raags, devotion, divine will, humility, and truthful conduct, the text offers a path rooted in remembrance of one universal creator and liberation from ego.

This episode traces how Sikh sacred verse became a roadmap for spiritual life, challenging empty ritual and worldly attachment while pointing toward devotion, service, and union with the Infinite.
