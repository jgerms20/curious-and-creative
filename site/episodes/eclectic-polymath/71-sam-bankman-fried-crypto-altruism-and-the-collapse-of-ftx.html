About this episode

Sam Bankman-Fried was once sold as the face of responsible crypto: a brilliant, frugal, math-driven founder who wanted to get rich so he could give it all away. Then FTX collapsed, and that image unraveled into one of the most dramatic financial scandals of the digital age.

This episode looks at the rise and fall of FTX and Alameda Research, tracing how effective altruism, crypto speculation, venture capital hype, celebrity endorsements, and weak corporate oversight created the conditions for catastrophe. What looked like disciplined risk-taking was hiding a much deeper problem: customer money, private databases, backdoor code, and a financial structure built on trust it had not earned.
