About this episode

Two decades in, podcasting still cannot agree on what a listener is. This episode is a deep dive into the business of podcasting and the future of audio itself: where the industry stands, why it is still being formed, and where it goes over the next ten, twenty, fifty, and one hundred years.

The business now: US podcast ad revenue hit $2.86 billion in 2025, the platform war between YouTube, Spotify, Apple, and now Netflix is reshaping the medium around video, and nine-figure deals have turned podcasters into moguls. Underneath, the industry still runs on an open RSS standard from 2001, measurement systems that cannot deduplicate one listener across platforms, and a legal frontier being written in real time, from state voice-rights laws in Tennessee and California to the proposed federal NO FAKES Act and the EU AI Act's rules on cloned voices.
