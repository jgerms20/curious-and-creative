In one line
Kindling is a card game for kindling and rekindling intimacy, with questions, touch, dares, dates, and an 18+ pack for exploring kink, all dialed from heat 1 to 5 with consent built into every rule.

Made by Joshua German and Janel Moore of Curious & Creative. We built it because the intimacy decks we owned were either all talk or all gimmick, and none of them were written for every kind of relationship. Kindling is queer by default, playable by couples, throuples, and friends, and scored so the winner's prize is always something the whole table enjoys.
