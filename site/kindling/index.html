Learn it in two minutes.

The rules fit on four cards in the box. Here they are.

01

Set the heat

Everyone secretly picks a max heat, 1 to 5. The table plays at the lowest one. Choose your decks and how long you're playing.

02

Draw & read

On your turn, draw and read the card out loud. Questions are for you to answer. Actions say who does what.

03

Choose

Do it and score. Claim it for later. Or skip it. Skipping is always free, with no explanation needed.

04

Score Sparks

The number in the corner is what it's worth. Embers cards are worth their heat. Expansion cards are worth double.

05

Match & wild

Match cards get a secret vote, and if everyone says yes, everyone scores double. Wild cards bend the rules.

06

Catch fire

First to 25 Sparks (or 50 on a long night) wins and picks a reward from their partner's wish list. Then you all go and do it.

Put money where your heart is.

The Pot is optional, and it's what turns one fun night into a month-long game. Everyone pays in, the Sparks leader decides how it's spent, and everyone goes.

$200in the pot this Season

How a Season works
  1. Pay in. Same amount each, into a jar, an envelope, or a shared account. Then together write a wish list of experiences you'd all love.
  2. Play for a month. Keep a running Sparks total across game nights. Woodpile cards you finish between games count too.
  3. Catch fire. At the end, the Sparks leader picks one experience from the wish list and the pot pays for it.
  4. Last place plans it. Bookings, reservations, the outfit reveal. Everyone goes. Everyone wins, and someone wins a little more.

Questions, answered.

Is Kindling only for couples?

No. Embers has 50+ "friend-safe" cards at heat 1–2 that work for friends or a first date. The digital game has a Friends mode that only deals those. There are also group cards written for three or more.

How spicy does it get?

As spicy as your table wants. Heat 1 is "describe their laugh." Heat 5 runs from "have sex somewhere semi-public you can actually get away with" to the 18+ After Dark pack. The table always plays at its lowest chosen heat, so you only get as far as everyone wants.

What's in After Dark?

60 cards about kink, power exchange, sensation, and roleplay, for couples and groups. It starts with negotiation cards (yes/no/maybe lists, safewords, traffic lights) and every card carries its own aftercare line. It's sold separately and is 18+ only.

Do we have to play for money?

Not at all. The Pot is optional. Without it, the winner picks one reward from a wish list their partner wrote before the game, and the partner gets to give it.
