About the show

There’s too much interesting stuff in the world to only care about one thing.

The Eclectic Polymath Podcast explores a different curiosity every episode: fascinating people, strange moments in history, cultural shifts, political movements, religions, technologies, music, myths, ideas, and questions you may never have thought to ask.

Some episodes explain how something became the way it is. Others unpack the life of someone worth knowing, trace an idea across centuries, or follow one rabbit hole until it connects to five others.

You don’t need to be an expert. You just need to stay curious.
