Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to hear back?

We review all inquiries within 48 hours. For specific project or partnership requests, we may need additional time to discuss internally before responding.

What types of projects do you take on?

We work across film, audio, digital content, interactive experiences, and branded projects. We're most interested in work that centers authentic stories and cultural perspectives.

How do I join the creative network?

Creatives interested in collaborating with C&C can submit their portfolio and information through the contact form. Indicate "I'd like to join the creative network" as your inquiry type.

Is there an incubator program?

Yes! We support emerging creators through mentorship, production resources, and revenue-sharing opportunities. Details and applications available upon inquiry.

Do you do consulting or advisory work?

We occasionally advise on creative strategy and cultural storytelling. Contact us to discuss potential consulting engagements.

What's your budget range?

We work across budgets, from micro-budget documentaries to larger production partnerships. Every project is unique—let's talk about what's possible for your vision.
