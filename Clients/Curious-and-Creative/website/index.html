About Curious & Creative

We are a creative studio and artist collective forging bold narratives at the intersection of culture, technology, and storytelling.

Inspired by the trailblazing work of studios like Proximity Media, Monkeypaw Productions, and HOORAE, we've built a unique model that combines a lean core team with a global network of writers, directors, animators, and cultural strategists.

Our mission: Champion bold new voices and amplify stories that shift culture.
