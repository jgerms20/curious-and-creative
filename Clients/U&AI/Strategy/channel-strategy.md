# U&AI Channel Strategy

## Overview
10-channel content strategy focused on founder personality, tactical business content, and platform-specific engagement.

**Monthly Budget:** $5,000
**Target Audience:** Business owners who need AI translated into action

---

## Channel Breakdown

### 1. TikTok
**Purpose:** Founder personality, behind-the-scenes, conference clips
**Tone:** Authentic, relatable, subtitled videos
**Content:**
- Eden & Yarden building the business
- Talking about AI and visibility
- Conference clips with subtitles
- Well-edited with personality

**Frequency:** 3-5x per week
**Budget Allocation:** 25% ($1,250)

---

### 2. Instagram
**Purpose:** Mix of personality and professional
**Tone:** Visual, aspirational, founder-focused
**Content:**
- Reels (repurposed TikTok)
- Stories showing day-to-day
- Helpful tips and insights
- Founder personality moments

**Frequency:** 4-5x per week
**Budget Allocation:** Part of Meta 30%

---

### 3. Facebook
**Purpose:** Converter channel for millennials/boomers
**Tone:** FOMO-driven, professional, results-focused
**Content:**
- Curated posts showing results
- Business impact stories
- Get them on the train messaging
- Make AI relevant to them

**Frequency:** 3x per week
**Budget Allocation:** Part of Meta 30%

---

### 4. X (Twitter)
**Purpose:** Response platform, thought leadership
**Tone:** Text-heavy, conversational, responsive
**Content:**
- Engage in AI conversations
- Reply to relevant threads
- Share insights and takes
- Be present in the space

**Frequency:** Daily engagement
**Budget Allocation:** Organic only

---

### 5. LinkedIn
**Purpose:** B2B presence, thought leadership
**Tone:** Professional, solutions-focused
**Content:**
- In-mail from founders (Eden/Yarden)
- Not company page posts
- Direct outreach strategy

**Frequency:** 2-3x per week
**Budget Allocation:** Organic + potential paid in-mail

---

### 6. Newsletter
**Purpose:** Direct engagement with audience
**Tone:** Personal, tactical, actionable
**Content:**
- What AI news means for your business
- Show founder personalities
- Tactical advice, not just news
- Fun facts about Eden/Yarden

**Frequency:** Weekly (Monday send)
**Platform:** Mailchimp

---

### 7. YouTube
**Purpose:** Long-form credibility content
**Tone:** Professional, authoritative
**Content:**
- Full conference talks
- "We did this at X event"
- Deep dives on visibility
- Credibility building

**Frequency:** 1-2x per month
**Budget Allocation:** 20% ($1,000)

---

### 8. YouTube Shorts
**Purpose:** Repurpose TikTok content
**Tone:** Same as TikTok
**Content:**
- Direct reposts from TikTok
- Clips from long-form YouTube

**Frequency:** 3-5x per week
**Budget Allocation:** Part of YouTube 20%

---

### 9. Podcasts
**Purpose:** Guest appearances for visibility
**Tone:** Expert, thought leader
**Strategy:**
- Pitch Eden/Yarden as guests
- Topics: AI visibility, future of search, brand presence
- Target: Business/marketing podcasts

**Frequency:** 1-2 appearances per month
**Budget Allocation:** Pitch time only

---

### 10. Guerilla Marketing
**Purpose:** Random acts of marketing
**Tone:** Creative, surprising, memorable
**Ideas:**
- Flyers at WeWork locations
- Billboards in key areas
- Unexpected touchpoints
- Conference presence

**Budget Allocation:** 10% ($500)

---

## Channels NOT Active
- Snapchat (not relevant)
- Threads (not performing well)
- Pinterest (ads only, maybe later)
- Reddit (ads only, maybe later)

---

## What We Do / Don't Do

### DO
- Lead with founder personality
- Make AI accessible and actionable
- Show results and case studies
- Engage authentically in conversations
- Respond to trends quickly

### DON'T
- Be overly corporate
- Use too much jargon
- Post without subtitles on video
- Ignore comments and engagement
- Be salesy in organic content
