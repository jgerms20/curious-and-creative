## Executive Summary

U&AI is a **done-for-you AI presence solution** that helps brands appear and perform well on AI platforms (ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, Meta AI). They position themselves as the bridge between traditional marketing and the emerging AI discovery layer where consumers increasingly make purchasing decisions.

The company is a product of **BRIKMAN Advertising**, an award-winning creative agency based in Las Vegas/West Hollywood, giving them strong creative and strategic DNA.

---

## Company Overview

### What They Do
U&AI helps brands:
- **Show up** on AI platforms when consumers ask relevant questions
- **Monitor sentiment** and how AI describes/recommends their brand
- **Track conversions** from AI-generated referrals
- **Build authority** that AI systems recognize and trust

### Core Value Proposition
> "Business owners shouldn't be AI experts to be present."

They position AI visibility as the next evolution beyond SEO - while SEO helps you rank on search engines, U&AI focuses on how brands appear *inside* AI responses where "rankings don't exist - only references, relevance, and trust."

### Differentiation
- Run **thousands of structured prompts daily** across multiple AI engines
- Simulate user intents tied to brand, category, and competitors
- Create **multi-dimensional visibility maps** analyzing frequency, sentiment, source attribution, and competitor proximity
- Results typically visible within **30-45 days**

## Competitive Positioning

"AI Visibility" / "AI Presence Management" - emerging category

### Positioning Statement (Inferred)
> For established brands who want to be discovered through AI platforms, U&AI is the done-for-you AI presence solution that ensures your brand shows up accurately and favorably in AI-generated recommendations, because traditional SEO doesn't reach where consumers are increasingly making decisions.

### Key Differentiators
1. **Creative agency heritage** - Not just tech; combines data with narrative strategy
2. **Done-for-you model** - Full service, dedicated client manager on every account
3. **Multi-platform monitoring** - Tracks across ChatGPT, Google AI Overview, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, Meta AI
4. **Conversion tracking** - Connects AI visibility to actual business outcomes
