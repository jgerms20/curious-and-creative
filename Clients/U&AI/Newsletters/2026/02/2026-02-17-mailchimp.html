AI Coworkers Are Here

Anthropic just released Claude Opus 4.6 with Agent Teams. Multiple AI agents working together. A 1 million token context window. And it triggered a $285 billion software stock selloff. Here's what happened.

What Changed

Claude Opus 4.6 launched last week with a feature nobody saw coming: Agent Teams. You can now spin up multiple AI agents that work simultaneously on different parts of a project.

Need a website built? One agent handles design, another writes code, a third debugs. They collaborate in real-time. No handoffs. No waiting.

The 1 million token context window means Claude can now hold 750,000 words in memory—enough to process entire codebases, documentation sets, or research libraries at once.

The Market Reacted Immediately

Within 48 hours of the announcement, software stocks dropped $285 billion in market value. Thomson Reuters fell 15.83%. Salesforce is down 25% year-to-date.

Investors are calling it the "SaaSpocalypse." The fear? That AI agents will replace entire categories of software tools—project management, CRM, analytics platforms—with natural language interfaces.

The Numbers Behind the Shift

Claude Code (the developer tool powered by Claude) hit a $1 billion annual run rate in just 6 months. Faster than Slack. Faster than Dropbox. Faster than almost any SaaS product in history.

Enterprise adoption is surging: 44% of enterprises now use Anthropic in production. That's up from 12% a year ago.

Developers are calling this the "vibe coding" era. You describe what you want. AI handles entire projects—not just snippets of code. Full features. Complete workflows.

What This Actually Means

AI coworkers aren't a future concept anymore. They're shipping. Companies are deploying them. The tools exist today.

This is the moment where AI stopped being a research curiosity and became infrastructure. Like the internet in 1995. Like mobile in 2008.

What This Means for Your Brand

AI coworkers are real now—not future speculation. This isn't a demo or a concept. Companies are using Agent Teams in production today. The shift is happening.

The $285B selloff shows investors believe traditional software is at risk. When markets react this violently, it signals a fundamental platform shift. The way people search for and evaluate solutions is changing.

Brands need AI strategy now, not later. Your customers are already using AI to research products, compare options, and make decisions. If you're not showing up in those conversations, you're invisible. U&AI helps businesses navigate this shift.
