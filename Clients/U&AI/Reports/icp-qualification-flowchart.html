Quick Reference

Qualifiers

  • Revenue $1M+/year
  • Ad budget $10K+/month (or willing)
  • Google/search dependent
  • Sales cycle < 6 months
  • Recurring revenue or high frequency
  • Competitive market

Disqualifiers

  • Restaurants (low margins, turnover)
  • Average deal < $3K
  • No recurring revenue AND low frequency
  • 9+ month sales cycles
  • Heavy regulation without process
