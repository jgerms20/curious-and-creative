U&AI Brand Voice

Voice & Tone Guidelines | February 2026

Overview

U&AI helps brands become visible on AI platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, etc.). Their clients are often business owners who aren't AI-native and need a trusted guide to navigate this emerging channel.

Voice Characteristics

Confident

They know their stuff, but they're here to help, not lecture. Never arrogant.

Professional

B2B service, serious about results. Business-appropriate language.

Solutions-Focused

Not fear-mongering about AI, but showing opportunity. Positive framing.

Accessible

Making complex AI concepts digestible for non-technical audiences.

Target Audience

Primary Audience Profile

Business owners, often Boomers/Gen X, who:

  • Know AI is important but don't have time to keep up
  • Need someone to translate "what's happening" into "what should I do"
  • Value expertise and results over buzzwords
  • Want a trusted partner, not a vendor

Writing Guidelines

Do

  • Lead with the business impact, not the technology
  • Use concrete examples and results
  • Explain jargon when used (or avoid it)
  • Frame AI as opportunity, not threat
  • Be direct and concise

Don't

  • Use excessive AI hype language
  • Assume technical knowledge
  • Talk down to the reader
  • Use fear-based messaging
  • Overload with statistics

Example Transformations

Example 1
"Leveraging LLM optimization strategies to enhance AI-driven discoverability"
"Making sure your business shows up when customers ask AI for recommendations"
Example 2
"The paradigm shift in search behavior necessitates proactive visibility management"
"People are asking ChatGPT instead of Googling. You need to be in those answers."

Newsletter Guidelines

Weekly AI Digest Approach

  • Open with context — What happened this week and why it matters
  • Always include "what this means for you" — Translate every headline into business impact
  • Keep it scannable — Busy executives, use headers and bullet points
  • End with outlook — What to watch for next week/month
  • Maintain positioning — U&AI is the guide who keeps clients informed
