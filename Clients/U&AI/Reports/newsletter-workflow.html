Newsletter Workflow

Weekly AI Digest System for U&AI | February 2026

Overview

A repeatable workflow for creating weekly AI news digests. Sources from trusted podcasts, applies U&AI's brand voice, and outputs formatted newsletters ready for distribution to their ~350 subscribers.

Frequency

Weekly — Every Friday/Saturday for Monday distribution

Audience

~350 subscribers — Business owners who want AI news simplified

Registered Sources

Content is pulled from these trusted sources, prioritized by relevance:

The Artificial Intelligence Show

Primary podcast source • Weekly episodes analyzing major AI developments

Weekly

AI Daily Brief

Daily AI news • Consolidated weekly for the digest

Daily

Additional web searches supplement these core sources with breaking news and relevant developments.

Workflow Steps

1

Content Gathering

Pull latest episodes/articles from registered sources. Web search for breaking AI news. Log everything with dates and sources to content-log.json.

2

Content Consolidation

Summarize key points from each source. Identify overlapping themes. Prioritize by relevance to U&AI's audience (business owners, non-technical).

3

Brand Voice Application

Load U&AI's brand voice guidelines. Rewrite in their voice: confident, professional, accessible. Add "what this means for your business" context.

4

Newsletter Assembly

Format into template with sections: Headlines, Deep Dive, Quick Hits, Looking Ahead. Add source citations. Generate both .md and .html versions.

Brand Voice

U&AI Newsletter Voice

The newsletter is the "voice of reason" for business owners who don't have time to keep up with AI news but need to understand what matters.

Confident Clear POV, no hedging
Professional Business-appropriate
Accessible No jargon, clear explanations
Solutions-Focused "Here's what to do"
