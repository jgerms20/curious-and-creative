Lead Quality Improvements

Budget allocation alone won't fix lead quality. These changes are essential:

Targeting Fixes

  1. Exclude low-intent audiences — Remove broad interests, tighten demographics
  2. Add negative keywords — "free", "cheap", "DIY", "course", "tutorial"
  3. Qualify in the ad — Mention pricing tier in copy ("starting at $X")
  4. Use lead forms with qualifiers — Company size, budget range questions
  5. Retargeting priority — Spend more on warm audiences, less on cold

Conversion Tracking

  • Set up proper pixel tracking on all platforms
  • Track form submissions AND qualified leads separately
  • Connect Attio to ad platforms for offline conversion tracking
  • Build lookalike audiences from closed deals, not just leads
