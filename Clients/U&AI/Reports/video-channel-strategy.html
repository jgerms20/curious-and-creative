Where to Deploy 5 Video Assets for Maximum Impact | February 2026

Executive Summary

Primary Recommendation

YouTube Pre-Roll + Meta Reels — Run "Car" and "Shopper" spots on YouTube for intent-based discovery, repurpose all 5 videos as Reels/Shorts for social reach. Allocate 60% of video budget to YouTube, 40% to Meta.

U&AI has 5 commercial video assets that are currently underutilized. Only search ads are running despite having strong creative. This strategy outlines where each video should run, in what format, and why.

Key Insight from Eden Call

"Car" and "Shopper" videos have historically performed best. These should be primary spend, with other videos used for testing and retargeting.

Channel-by-Channel Strategy

YouTube Pre-Roll

  • Videos: Car, Shopper
  • Format: 15-30 second skippable
  • Targeting: Business owners, entrepreneurs, "AI for business" searchers
  • Goal: Awareness + website traffic

YouTube Shorts

  • Videos: All 5 (cropped to vertical)
  • Format: Under 60 seconds, vertical
  • Targeting: Broad discovery
  • Goal: Reach + brand awareness

Meta (Facebook/Instagram)

  • Videos: Car (feed), Shopper (Reels)
  • Format: Square for feed, vertical for Reels
  • Targeting: SMB owners, decision makers
  • Goal: Leads + retargeting pool

LinkedIn (Organic First)

  • Videos: Conference footage, founder clips
  • Format: Native upload, captions
  • Targeting: B2B decision makers
  • Goal: Credibility + thought leadership

TikTok Consideration

TikTok is lower priority for B2B services, but can work for brand awareness and attracting younger business owners. Post all 5 videos organically first to test engagement before paid spend.

Implementation Phases

Phase 1: Foundation (Week 1)

  • Upload all 5 videos to Google Ads (YouTube)
  • Create 15-second and 30-second cuts
  • Set up YouTube pre-roll campaign with Car + Shopper
  • Upload vertical versions to Shorts

Phase 2: Meta Launch (Week 2)

  • Upload to Meta Business Manager
  • Create feed and Reels versions
  • Set up lookalike audiences from existing leads
  • Launch Car video in feed, Shopper in Reels

Phase 3: Optimization (Weeks 3-4)

  • Review performance data
  • Pause underperformers, scale winners
  • Introduce Video #4 for retargeting
  • Test LinkedIn paid with conference clips

Phase 4: Scale (Month 2+)

  • Rotate in Video #5 for fresh creative
  • Expand targeting based on learnings
  • Consider TikTok paid if organic shows promise
  • Create new video content based on top performer style

Creative Recommendations

Video Edits Needed

  1. Vertical crops — All 5 videos need 9:16 versions for Shorts/Reels/TikTok
  2. 15-second cuts — For YouTube bumper ads and Instagram Stories
  3. Caption overlays — 85% of video is watched on mute
  4. Strong hook — First 3 seconds must grab attention (recut if needed)

A/B Testing Plan

  • Test 1: Car vs. Shopper on YouTube (which drives more clicks?)
  • Test 2: 15-second vs. 30-second on Meta (completion rates)
  • Test 3: With captions vs. without on Instagram Reels
  • Test 4: Feed placement vs. Reels placement on Meta
