U&AI Differentiation Opportunities

1. Lead with Creative, Not Data

The problem: Most competitors are SEO agencies or software companies. They lead with dashboards and metrics.

U&AI's edge: BRIKMAN's creative heritage. The story matters as much as the visibility. U&AI can shape the narrative AI tells about a brand, not just track it.

"We don't just get you visible. We make sure AI tells your story right."

2. Done-For-You for Non-Technical Clients

The problem: Software tools require clients to act. Traditional agencies require clients to review decks and approve strategies.

U&AI's edge: Position for business owners who don't want to learn AI. Fully managed. Dedicated account manager.

"You don't need to understand AI. You just need to show up in it."

3. Multi-Platform Native (Not Just ChatGPT)

The problem: Many agencies still lead with "ChatGPT optimization" as if it's the only AI.

U&AI's edge: Already monitoring ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI. Position as the only team that covers everywhere customers are asking.

"Your customers aren't just on ChatGPT. Neither are we."

4. Results in 30-45 Days (Speed)

The problem: Traditional SEO takes 6-12 months. AI visibility can shift faster.

U&AI's edge: Already claiming 30-45 day timelines. This is a major differentiator if they can prove it.

"Visible in 30 days. Not 12 months."

GEO, AEO, AI SEO, AI visibility... the terminology is fragmented. U&AI needs to pick a term and own it.
