Strategic Reasoning

Meta: Proven Conversion Engine

Facebook and Instagram have demonstrated the highest ROI for lead generation and conversion. The 30% allocation reflects their effectiveness at reaching millennials and boomers with FOMO-driven messaging and retargeting capabilities. This is where business owners make decisions.

TikTok: Personality at Scale

TikTok's algorithm rewards authentic founder content and can rapidly scale reach. The 25% budget investment recognizes the platform's unique ability to showcase personality while building a following that translates to other channels. Raw beats polished here.

YouTube: Long-Form Credibility

YouTube builds lasting credibility through conference talks and tutorials. The 20% allocation supports both long-form content creation and short-form distribution via Shorts. This platform converts skeptics into believers through depth and expertise.

Guerilla: Surprise & Delight

The 10% guerilla budget creates unexpected brand moments that generate organic conversation. Whether it's WeWork flyers or strategic billboards, these tactics cut through digital noise with physical presence and memorable experiences that people talk about.

Multi-Channel Presence

The remaining 15% distributed across LinkedIn, X, newsletters, and podcasts ensures U&AI maintains presence where business owners consume content. Each channel serves a specific purpose in the buyer journey from awareness to conversion.

Founder-Led Approach

Every channel strategy centers on founder authenticity. Business owners buy from people, not faceless agencies. The budget allocation prioritizes platforms where personality shines and founders can connect directly with their audience.

Channel Guidelines: Do's and Don'ts

Do's

TikTok & Instagram

  • Post raw, unedited founder moments
  • Show faces, personalities, emotions
  • Jump on trending audio quickly
  • Use captions for accessibility
  • Engage with comments within 1 hour

Facebook

  • Use clear calls-to-action
  • Test multiple ad variations
  • Retarget website visitors
  • Create FOMO with limited offers
  • Build community in groups

X (Twitter)

  • Respond within minutes when possible
  • Share hot takes on industry news
  • Use threads for complex ideas
  • Engage with bigger accounts
  • Be conversational and human

LinkedIn

  • Write long-form thought leadership
  • Personalize InMail outreach
  • Share conference appearances
  • Highlight client wins (with permission)
  • Maintain professional tone

YouTube

  • Optimize titles and thumbnails
  • Provide value in first 30 seconds
  • Include timestamps for long videos
  • Cross-promote on other channels
  • Create playlists by topic

Don'ts

TikTok & Instagram

  • Over-polish content (looks fake)
  • Ignore negative comments
  • Post without captions
  • Use outdated trends
  • Focus only on selling

Facebook

  • Send cold traffic to sales pages
  • Use clickbait headlines
  • Ignore ad fatigue metrics
  • Post without testing
  • Spam groups with promotions

X (Twitter)

  • Auto-DM new followers
  • Tweet only links (no context)
  • Argue with trolls publicly
  • Use too many hashtags
  • Post and ghost

LinkedIn

  • Send generic connection requests
  • Pitch immediately after connecting
  • Over-share personal content
  • Use LinkedIn like Facebook
  • Ignore InMail responses

YouTube

  • Upload without keyword research
  • Neglect video descriptions
  • Forget end screens and cards
  • Ignore audience retention data
  • Post inconsistently

Universal Do's (All Channels)

  • Lead with founder personality and authenticity
  • Provide tactical, actionable value
  • Respond to engagement quickly
  • Test and iterate based on data
  • Repurpose content across platforms
  • Track conversions, not just vanity metrics
  • Maintain consistent brand voice

Universal Don'ts (All Channels)

  • Post without purpose or strategy
  • Copy competitors without adding unique value
  • Ignore analytics and performance data
  • Be overly promotional or salesy
  • Neglect community engagement
  • Post inconsistently or sporadically
  • Forget to track ROI by channel
