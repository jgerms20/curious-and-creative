2. UTM Parameter Guide

What are UTM Parameters?

UTM parameters are tags you add to the end of URLs to track where traffic comes from. When someone clicks a link with UTMs, analytics tools (like Google Analytics) can tell you exactly which ad, campaign, or channel drove that visitor.

Why they matter: Without UTMs, all your paid traffic might show up as "direct" or "referral" in analytics. UTMs let you see which specific campaigns are generating leads and sales.

UTM Structure Breakdown:

  • utm_source = Where the traffic comes from (meta, google, tiktok, newsletter)
  • utm_medium = Type of traffic (paid, organic, email, social)
  • utm_campaign = Specific campaign name (cold_biz_owners, ai_marketing)
  • utm_content = Variation or ad creative (static_image, video_ad, carousel)
  • utm_term = Keyword for search ads (optional, mainly for Google Ads)

Platform-Specific UTM Examples:

Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram)
https://uandai.com/consultation?utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=cold_biz_owners&utm_content=static_image
  • utm_source=meta - Traffic from Meta/Facebook
  • utm_medium=paid - Paid advertising
  • utm_campaign=cold_biz_owners - Campaign targeting cold business owners
  • utm_content=static_image - Specific ad creative type

Where to use: In your Meta Ads Manager, paste this full URL in the "Website URL" field for each ad.

Google Ads
https://uandai.com/consultation?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=ai_marketing&utm_term=ai_marketing_agency
  • utm_source=google - Traffic from Google
  • utm_medium=cpc - Cost-per-click advertising
  • utm_campaign=ai_marketing - AI marketing campaign
  • utm_term=ai_marketing_agency - Keyword that triggered the ad

Where to use: In Google Ads, add this to the "Final URL" field or use auto-tagging (enable in account settings).

TikTok Ads
https://uandai.com/consultation?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=business_owners&utm_content=video_hook1
  • utm_source=tiktok - Traffic from TikTok
  • utm_medium=paid - Paid advertising
  • utm_campaign=business_owners - Campaign targeting business owners
  • utm_content=video_hook1 - First video hook variation

Where to use: In TikTok Ads Manager, paste in the "Destination URL" field.

LinkedIn Ads
https://uandai.com/consultation?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=b2b_decision_makers&utm_content=carousel
  • utm_source=linkedin - Traffic from LinkedIn
  • utm_medium=paid - Paid advertising
  • utm_campaign=b2b_decision_makers - Campaign targeting B2B decision makers
  • utm_content=carousel - Carousel ad format

Where to use: In LinkedIn Campaign Manager, add to the "Destination URL" field.

Newsletter / Email
https://uandai.com/consultation?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_feb16
  • utm_source=newsletter - Traffic from newsletter
  • utm_medium=email - Email channel
  • utm_campaign=weekly_feb16 - Specific newsletter edition

Where to use: Add to any links in your email newsletters or campaigns.

Pro Tip: UTM Builder

Use Google's free Campaign URL Builder to generate UTM links easily: ga-dev-tools.google/campaign-url-builder/

Or create a simple spreadsheet with formulas to auto-generate UTM URLs for all your campaigns.

3. Status Workflow Visual

Move leads through these stages as you nurture them:

New
Lead just came in
→
Contacted
First outreach sent
→
Qualified
Good fit confirmed
→
Meeting Booked
Call scheduled
→
Closed Won/Lost
Final outcome

Expected Actions at Each Stage:

New:
  • Log lead in Google Sheet with all details
  • Review source/campaign to understand context
  • Research company/person if B2B
Contacted:
  • Send personalized intro email within 24 hours
  • Follow up via phone if number provided
  • Note contact method and response in Notes field
Qualified:
  • Confirm budget, timeline, and decision-making authority
  • Assess if services align with their needs
  • Propose next steps (discovery call, proposal)
Meeting Booked:
  • Send calendar invite with Zoom/meeting link
  • Prepare custom agenda based on their needs
  • Send reminder 24 hours before call
Closed Won/Lost:
  • Won: Send contract, onboard client, celebrate!
  • Lost: Note reason in Notes, add to nurture list for future
  • Both: Update source performance data for optimization
