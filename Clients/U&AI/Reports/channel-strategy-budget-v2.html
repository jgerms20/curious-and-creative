💡 Recommendations

1

Prioritize Google Ads for High-Intent Leads

With $1,000/month, focus on bottom-of-funnel keywords like "AI marketing agency" and "AI visibility consultant". These searchers are actively looking for solutions—capture them before competitors.

2

Use TikTok Spark Ads to Amplify Winners

Don't create separate ad content. Identify organic posts that perform well (5K+ views), then boost them with Spark Ads. This maintains authenticity while scaling reach.

3

Test Meta Advantage+ Before Manual Campaigns

Meta's AI-driven Advantage+ campaigns consistently outperform manual targeting by 2x. Start here, then layer in manual campaigns for specific audiences.

4

Week 3: Evaluate & Reallocate

After 2 weeks of data, identify your best-performing channel. Consider shifting 20% budget from lowest performer to highest. Track cost-per-lead, not just impressions.
