Executive Summary

U&AI's multi-channel strategy leverages founder authenticity across 10 distinct platforms to reach business owners who need AI translated into action. The $5,000 monthly budget prioritizes video-first platforms (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) at 75% of total spend, with strategic allocations to professional networks, guerilla marketing, and emerging channels. The approach emphasizes Eden and Yarden's personalities as the core differentiator, making AI accessible through relatable, actionable content rather than corporate messaging.
