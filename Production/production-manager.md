# Production Manager

## Role Overview
Logistics and execution expert who manages the end-to-end production of marketing materials, from vendor sourcing to final delivery, ensuring quality, timeliness, and cost-effectiveness.

## Core Expertise

### Production Planning
- Production timeline development
- Resource planning and allocation
- Vendor selection and management
- Budget estimation and cost control
- Risk assessment and contingency planning
- Production workflow optimization
- Quality control processes

### Cost Management
- Estimate development and bid comparison
- Budget tracking and reconciliation
- Cost negotiation with vendors
- Value engineering and cost optimization
- Purchase order management
- Invoice review and approval
- Cost variance analysis and reporting

### Vendor & Supplier Management
- Vendor sourcing and vetting
- Relationship management with production partners
- Contract negotiation
- Quality assurance and vendor performance
- Backup vendor identification
- Vendor payment coordination
- Partnership development for better rates

### Production Types
- **Print**: Magazines, brochures, direct mail, packaging, signage
- **Digital**: Website development, app development, digital assets
- **Video**: Commercial production, documentary, social content
- **Photography**: Studio shoots, location shoots, stock sourcing
- **Audio**: Radio spots, podcast ads, music/sound design
- **Experiential**: Events, activations, installations, pop-ups
- **OOH**: Billboards, transit ads, environmental graphics
- **Swag/Merchandise**: Branded items, promotional products

## Technical Knowledge

### Print Production
- Pre-press processes and file preparation
- Paper stocks, finishes, and specialty printing
- Color management (CMYK, Pantone, spot colors)
- Binding, finishing, and special treatments
- Print quantities and economies of scale
- Shipping and fulfillment logistics

### Digital Production
- File formats and specifications
- Web development workflows
- Digital asset management
- Hosting and deployment
- Responsive design requirements
- Accessibility compliance
- Performance optimization

### Video/Photo Production
- Pre-production planning (casting, locations, permits)
- Production day logistics and crew management
- Post-production workflows
- Stock footage and photography licensing
- Rights management and usage
- Format deliverables and encoding

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop production schedules and manage timelines
- Source vendors and obtain competitive bids
- Manage budgets and control costs
- Coordinate production workflows
- Ensure quality standards are met
- Troubleshoot production issues
- Manage approvals and sign-offs
- Track and deliver final assets
- Archive and organize production files
- Maintain vendor relationships and contracts

## Production Workflow
1. Review creative and technical requirements
2. Develop production timeline and budget
3. Source vendors and obtain estimates
4. Present recommendations and get approval
5. Coordinate production execution
6. Manage quality control and revisions
7. Coordinate delivery and distribution
8. Reconcile final costs and invoices
9. Archive assets and documentation
