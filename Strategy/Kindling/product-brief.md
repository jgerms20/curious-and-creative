# Kindling — Product Brief

## What it is

**Kindling** is a modular card game for rekindling and deepening connection.
It is built for couples, but designed to work for any pairing — partners,
new lovers, throuples, and groups exploring intimacy together. It draws
the line *We're Not Really Strangers* drew (vulnerability prompts), but
also reaches into physical intimacy, kink exploration, and shared
experiences in the way no other game on the shelf currently does — with
queer-inclusive language, explicit consent framing, and a cooperative
reward system instead of a competitive one.

## Why now

- The intimacy-deck category exists (WNRS, Intimacy Deck, BestSelf's
  *Intimacy Deck*) but is heavily talk-focused and skews straight-coded.
- Couples are looking for low-friction ways to break routine without
  paying for therapy or weekend retreats.
- Therapists, sex educators, and sex-positive podcasts (*Decisions
  Decisions*, *Sex With Emily*, *Where Should We Begin?*) need a
  product they can recommend that is thoughtful, consent-forward, and
  not gimmicky.

## Product principles

1. **Consent is the game, not a disclaimer.** Every card can be declined,
   for free, no penalty, no eyebrows raised.
2. **Pairings, not couples.** Language and mechanics work for two, three,
   or more.
3. **Queer by default.** No card assumes gender, anatomy, or relationship
   structure.
4. **Intimacy ≠ sex.** Half the base deck is non-physical. A bathtub and a
   book is a card.
5. **Cooperative reward, not competition.** The "winner" picks a *shared*
   reward both players already wanted.
6. **Beautiful object.** It should look like something you leave on the
   coffee table, not hide in a drawer.

## Audiences

- **Primary:** couples 25–45 who already buy "good design" objects and
  follow at least one relationship/intimacy creator.
- **Secondary:** queer pairings and throuples underserved by existing
  decks.
- **Tertiary:** sex-positive professionals (therapists, coaches,
  educators) who'll recommend it.

## Format

Base deck + modular add-on packs. See `game-design.md`.

## Success looks like

- 1,000-unit first run sells out within the first launch quarter.
- 30+ therapist / educator recommendations on launch.
- A clear sense that customers feel safer, not more performative, after
  playing.
