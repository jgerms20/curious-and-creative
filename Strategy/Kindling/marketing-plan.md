# Kindling — Marketing & Launch Plan

## Positioning line

> "Kindling is a card game for pairings — for the couples, throuples,
> and people who want to know each other more closely. Built consent-first,
> queer by default, designed to be played, not displayed."

---

## Audience segments

### 1. The "good-design couple"
- 25–45, urban or near-urban.
- Already owns *Cards Against Humanity*, *We're Not Really Strangers*,
  *The And* deck, or *Esther Perel*'s deck.
- Buys lifestyle objects from Goodee, Areaware, Block Shop.
- Reaches: Instagram, Substack, "things to buy" gift guides.

### 2. Queer pairings & throuples
- Underserved by current intimacy decks.
- Reaches: TikTok and Instagram queer creators, queer bookstores
  (Bluestockings, A Room of One's Own), zines, Autostraddle.

### 3. Sex-positive professionals
- Therapists, sex coaches, sex educators, dating coaches.
- They recommend products in sessions and on social.
- Reaches: 1:1 outreach, gifting, professional newsletters.

### 4. Gift-buyers
- Friends-of-couples buying for engagements, anniversaries,
  housewarmings, breakups-into-something-better.

---

## Brand voice for marketing

- Warm, dry, specific. "We made a thing for the people we love."
- Not coy, not sales-y, not therapeutic-clinical.
- Photo direction: hands, candles, sheets, real apartments, no models.

---

## Pre-launch (Months -3 to 0)

1. **Build the waitlist.** `/kindling` microsite ships with a simple
   email form. Goal: 2,000 emails before launch.
2. **Seed 50 advance copies** to a tight list:
   - 20 therapists / coaches / educators (see list below).
   - 15 creators (relationship, queer life, design).
   - 10 podcasts (see list below).
   - 5 journalists writing about sex, relationships, or design.
3. **Soft-launch newsletter.** Weekly notes on what we're learning while
   building — playtests, manufacturer visits, a card we cut. Published
   on the Journal section of the main site.

---

## Launch (Month 0)

1. **Open the store.** Pre-orders convert first.
2. **Day-of launch:** newsletter, partner posts go live the same day,
   gifted creators post within a 72-hour window.
3. **First press push:** one in-depth feature with a friendly outlet
   (target: *Cup of Jo*, *The Cut*, *Autostraddle*) plus a syndicated
   gift-guide push (target: *Apartment Therapy*, *The Strategist*).
4. **First podcast circuit** — see list below.

---

## Post-launch (Months 1-6)

- **Repeat purchase engine:** add-on packs are the repeat purchase. Email
  cadence: 2 weeks after a base-deck purchase, hit them with a "ready
  for more heat?" mailer for Challenges + Activities. 3 months later,
  hit them with the Kink Pack.
- **Therapist program:** offer therapists wholesale at 50% + a referral
  link.
- **Affiliate program** for creators: 15% of revenue on referrals.

### Outreach template

Short, warm, specific. Reference one episode. Offer a free advance copy
no-strings. Mention the consent-first design as the hook — that is the
thing that differentiates Kindling from every other deck they've seen
pitched to them this year.

## Why Kindling will work as a marketing story

- **It's not just a deck — it's a position.** Consent-first, queer by
  default, cooperative scoring. Every story we write *is* a story
  about how relationships could feel.
- **It has built-in shareable beats.** "What's on the card you couldn't
  do?" is a TikTok hook for the rest of time.
- **It's gift-shaped.** Anniversaries, engagements, housewarmings,
  Galentine's Day, Pride, holidays — six big moments a year.
