# Curious & Creative — Mission

**Curious & Creative LLC** is the holding company for the projects Jay and
Janelle make together. It is not an agency. It is not a service business.
It is a small studio for our curious, creative life — and a place to put
the things we build into the world.

## What we believe

- **Curiosity is a practice, not a personality trait.** It can be trained,
  and it gets sharper when shared with someone you love.
- **Creativity belongs to everyone.** We don't want to gatekeep it behind
  industries or job titles.
- **Intimacy is the same skill as creativity** — paying close attention,
  taking small risks, making room for surprise.
- **Play is serious work.** The best things we make are the ones that felt
  like games while we were making them.

## What lives here

- **The Podcast** — long-form conversations on living a curious life.
- **Kindling** — a card game for rekindling and deepening connection.
- **The Journal** — short writing, essays, and notes from the studio.
- Future projects — books, more games, events, whatever we get curious
  about next.

## What we're not

We're not building an agency, we're not taking client work, and we're not
trying to scale into a media company. We are two people making things we
believe in, together, with the door open for collaborators we love.
