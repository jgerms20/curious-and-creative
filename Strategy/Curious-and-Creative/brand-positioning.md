# Curious & Creative — Brand Positioning

## One-line positioning

A small studio for our curious creative life — the home for everything
Jay and Janelle make together.

## Voice

- **Warm but precise.** Atlantic-essay cadence, not corporate-deck cadence.
- **Honest, lightly funny.** Not jokey. Dry warmth, occasional grin.
- **First-person plural.** "We make." "We're curious about." Never "the brand."
- **Specific over generic.** A real anecdote beats a tagline.

## Inspirations (for site & writing)

- **The Ringer** — confident editorial layouts, voice-driven sections.
- **The Atlantic** — typography-led, idea-first, restrained color.
- **Cup of Jo** — warmth and small-life specificity.
- **Are.na** — a quiet, well-organized room for curiosities.

## Look & feel

- **Typography:** serif display (Fraunces or GT Sectra) + clean sans
  (Inter or General Sans).
- **Palette:**
  - Ivory `#F7F3EC`
  - Ink `#161513`
  - Terracotta accent `#C25A3C`
  - Sage neutral `#7E8B7A`
- **Layout:** asymmetric editorial grids, hairline rules, generous
  whitespace, large headlines.
- **Photography:** documentary, naturally lit, hands and small objects
  preferred over stock portraits.

## What this brand does not do

- Stock-photo team grids.
- "Solutions" / "synergy" / "leverage" language.
- Hustle-coded ALL-CAPS marketing.
- Pretending to be bigger than it is.
